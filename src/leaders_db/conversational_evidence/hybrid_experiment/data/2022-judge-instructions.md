# 2022 comparative judge calibration

Apply the chapter guide to the full 20-ruler cohort. Do not treat the cohort's worst
ruler as an automatic score of 1; the 1–10 scale is historical and absolute.

For Chapter 1B in particular, reserve score 1 for the guide's realized-catastrophe
floor: an actual nuclear detonation against people or a military target, or a genuinely
equivalent realized nuclear catastrophe. Nuclear threats, unsafe escalation, arms
buildup, aggression, authoritarianism, or even Nazi-regime-level repression do not by
themselves satisfy the Chapter 1B score-1 threshold. Severe but unrealized nuclear-risk
conduct normally belongs above 1, often in the 2–3 range depending on the evidence.

Use missing or weak ruler-attributed evidence to reduce confidence and trigger review,
not to infer either a low or high substantive score.
