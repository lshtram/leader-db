# Comparative evaluation

Scores assess artifact quality only. Overall scores are the arithmetic mean of the ten components.

Component key: **C1** coverage; **C2** source portfolio; **C3** inspection/locators; **C4** precision/period fit; **C5** attribution/baseline/shocks; **C6** balance; **C7** evidence-status distinctions; **C8** gap honesty; **C9** reusability/machine validity; **C10** efficiency.

## 1. Andrés Manuel López Obrador — Mexico, 5B

| Artifact | C1 | C2 | C3 | C4 | C5 | C6 | C7 | C8 | C9 | C10 | Overall |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| X | 8.5 | 8.5 | 8.0 | 9.0 | 9.0 | 8.5 | 9.0 | 9.0 | 8.0 | 7.0 | **8.5** |
| Y | 7.5 | 8.0 | 8.5 | 8.5 | 8.5 | 8.5 | 9.0 | 8.5 | 9.0 | 8.5 | **8.3** |

**Better artifact: X, narrowly.**

- X has materially better welfare and trajectory coverage: health-access deterioration, multidimensional poverty, formal employment, FDI concentration, depleted stabilization buffers, tax capacity and project-appraisal weaknesses. Its poverty/health contradiction is particularly chapter-useful.
- Attribution is excellent in both: Banxico tightening is separated from AMLO; the minimum-wage decision is treated as tripartite; reopening, U.S. demand and oil shocks are retained.
- X’s main defects are volume and a few weaker records: the minimum-wage and IMSS records are labelled fully extracted despite fetch limitations, AP is unnecessary for GDP when INEGI was the underlying authority, and OECD material is split into several overlapping facts.
- Y has cleaner machine engineering: explicit `inspection_state`, `source_dependencies`, `new_fact_contribution`, and a stronger official wage resolution. Its supplier-sanctions record is useful favorable counterevidence.
- Y’s efficiency gain does **not** fully compensate for losing health-access, FDI, formal-employment and fiscal-buffer evidence.
- Preserve from Y: the official CONASAMI resolution, dependency fields, procedural classification of the USMCA dispute, and supplier-sanctions counterevidence.

## 2. Joe Biden — United States, 6B

| Artifact | C1 | C2 | C3 | C4 | C5 | C6 | C7 | C8 | C9 | C10 | Overall |

**Better artifact: X, by a very small margin.**

- X has the more complete chapter portfolio: PACT Act, delivered PSLF relief, failed broad debt cancellation, reproductive access after *Dobbs*, homelessness, water, housing, food, education and health.
- It is especially strong at separating enactment, administrative processing and realized benefit. The broad debt plan is correctly recorded as producing no discharged debt; IRA provisions are not backdated into 2022 outcomes.

- Y also adds some weaker or marginal material: constitutional authority consumes an atomic record, EPA “expected projects” remain far from service outcomes, and several mortality records have weak presidential attribution.
- X is somewhat padded: the portfolio reaches into peripheral welfare domains, and several administrative announcements add limited causal value.
- Y’s modest efficiency gain only **partly** compensates for losing veterans, PSLF, reproductive-health and failed-debt-plan evidence.
- Preserve from Y: actual ESSER expenditure, the corrective OMB/GAO record, maternal-mortality distribution, accessible testing and its stronger inspection-state schema.

## 3. Jair Bolsonaro — Brazil, 7B

| Artifact | C1 | C2 | C3 | C4 | C5 | C6 | C7 | C8 | C9 | C10 | Overall |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| Y | 7.5 | 8.0 | 7.5 | 8.0 | 8.0 | 8.0 | 8.5 | 8.0 | 8.5 | 8.0 | **8.0** |

**Better artifact: X, clearly.**

- X uses later adjudication far more effectively. The final coup-plot judgment is explicitly retrospective, procedurally final and tied to 2022 conduct; Y knowingly stops at the earlier police allegation despite identifying later official judgment pages.
- X also supplies major missing dimensions: the Silveira pardon, RP-9, hunger denial, family-access secrecy, annual accounts, appointee conduct and the favorable Covaxin disposition.
- Both distinguish allegations from findings well. X is particularly careful not to convert jewelry, property purchases, RP-9 or family allegations into proven personal enrichment.

- Y is more compact and has useful direct evidence of failure to attend an ordered police interview and vaccine-record opacity.
- Its efficiency gain does **not** compensate for omitting decisive final adjudication and several directly attributable uses of presidential power.
- Preserve from Y: the interview-nonattendance record, vaccine-record transparency decision and its restrained treatment of the gifts inquiry.

## 5. Nguyễn Phú Trọng — Vietnam, 8B

| Y | 9.0 | 8.5 | 8.5 | 8.5 | 8.5 | 8.5 | 8.5 | 8.5 | 9.0 | 8.0 | **8.6** |

**Better artifact: Y.**

- Y more fully freezes the program before judging outcomes and then connects it to a measurable government plan, recovery allocations, provincial committees, case deadlines and public-investment correction machinery.
- Its best analytical contribution is the cross-program tradeoff: anti-corruption strengthened coercive implementers while contributing to risk aversion, medical-supply problems and weak public-investment execution.
- Y’s macro record is also superior: independent IMF results include growth, inflation, financial stress and the bank-run response rather than relying mainly on official GDP.
- X has good senior-removal evidence and a useful World Bank structural-coordination diagnosis, but several records have weaker extraction: the World Bank landing-page claim and official enforcement totals receive more weight than their inspection state warrants.
- Both appropriately limit personal attribution for economic administration and appointments.
- Y is not materially shorter, so its advantage is better evidence engineering rather than token savings.
- Preserve from X: explicit senior-official removals, the independent high-income implementation diagnosis and the clearer warning that enforcement counts do not measure corruption prevalence.

# Overall recommendation

## Case winners

| Case | Better artifact |
|---|---|
| AMLO | X |
| Biden | X, narrowly |
| Bolsonaro | X |
| Hasina | X |
| Nguyễn Phú Trọng | Y |
| Putin | Y, narrowly |
| Scholz | X |
| Sisi | X, marginally |
| Tshisekedi | X |
| Xi | Y |

Artifact X is stronger in seven cases, principally because it more often preserves material domain coverage, decisive later adjudication, ruler-specific contrary evidence and end-state evidence. Artifact Y is strongest where it improves record schemas, inspection-state honesty, source dependencies and analytical compression—most clearly for Nguyễn Phú Trọng and Xi.

**Do not promote Y wholesale.** Its efficiency improvement is inconsistent and, in several cases, comes from removing material evidence rather than deduplicating genuinely repetitive material. That fails the requirement of no meaningful quality regression.

The best path is a hybrid:

- Retain X’s substantive research depth and wider adverse-case search.
- Adopt Y’s `inspection_state`, `new_fact_contribution`, `source_dependencies` and evidence-status fields.
- Require partial, indexed-only and access-limited sources to remain explicitly non-fully-extracted.
- Consolidate records drawn from the same report when they do not add genuinely independent facts.
- Preserve Y’s strongest additions: official underlying legal records, actual implementation rather than authorization, corrective-action evidence, and explicit dependency tracking.

That combination would improve evidence engineering and efficiency without sacrificing the material coverage that makes X stronger overall.
