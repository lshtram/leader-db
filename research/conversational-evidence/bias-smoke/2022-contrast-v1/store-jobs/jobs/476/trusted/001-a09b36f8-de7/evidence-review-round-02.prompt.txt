This is strong target-year safeguards evidence, but attribution is institutional rather than personal; no Støre direction is shown. [DSA, “Safeguardsinspeksjon 2022,” updated 22 June 2022](https://www.dsa.no/nyheter/safeguardsinspeksjon-2022) (lines 79–86).

This is target-period government-policy evidence, not a personal Støre statement. [Government.no, “The Modern-Day Nuclear Threat and How to Respond,” 8 June 2022](https://www.regjeringen.no/no/whats-new/nuclear_threat/id2918066/) (lines 176–210).

This supplies adverse scrutiny plus the government response, but attribution is to the cabinet/foreign minister, not specifically Støre. [Storting, Document 15:71 (2022–2023)](https://www.stortinget.no/no/Saker-og-publikasjoner/Sporsmal/Skriftlige-sporsmal-og-svar/Skriftlig-sporsmal/?qnid=76644) (lines 296–324).

It also records Norway’s attendance as an observer at the first TPNW meeting while declining signature because of NATO commitments. This is retrospective official evidence of target-year implementation, with cabinet-level rather than personal attribution.
