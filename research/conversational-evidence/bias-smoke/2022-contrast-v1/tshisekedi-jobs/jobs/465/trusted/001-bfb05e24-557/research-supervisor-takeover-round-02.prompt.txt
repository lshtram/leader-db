## Chapter yield

- **1B:** Local package had no retained nuclear/existential-risk facts. Web research found standing nonproliferation safeguards and a 2022 cybersecurity strategy, but no accepted evidence of nuclear weapons authority, nuclear coercion, proliferation, or catastrophic-risk use. Cyber governance is the only plausible target-period exposure found.
- **2B:** Strongest chapter yield. Local UCDP/SIPRI facts establish conflict exposure and military burden, not personal responsibility. E005 and E010–E013 add attribution-separated armed-conflict, regional diplomacy, threat-justification, and peace-process evidence.
- **3B:** Strong target-period evidence concerning state-of-siege restrictions, military courts, arrests, protests, journalists, and civilian harm. Attribution is strongest for national policy and state forces; nonstate and decentralized abuses remain separately identified.
- **4B:** Evidence covers CENI design, gubernatorial elections, vote-buying allegations, media restrictions, assembly restrictions, and internet conditions. The electoral evidence is mixed and includes both peaceful elections/observer access and institutional-independence concerns.
- **5B:** IMF provides the clearest ruler-period policy and implementation evidence. Mining-governance evidence adds a direct state-policy and audit trail but not personal enrichment. Local macro outcomes remain country context.
- **6B:** Education policy and implementation evidence is usable, but donor dependence, conflict, displacement, access gaps, and quality/sustainability limits are material. Local health and HDI facts remain outcome context.
- **7B:** Personal-nexus evidence is thin. The Gécamines audit and adviser episode are relevant but do not establish personal benefit by Tshisekedi. Official judicial and anticorruption actions are evidence of action, not proof of integrity or independence.
- **8B:** Program evidence exists for infrastructure, education, macroeconomic reform, cybersecurity, judicial reform, and regional security diplomacy. Implementation is mixed and constrained; BTI is useful but partly retrospective and not a substitute for 2022 administrative records.
