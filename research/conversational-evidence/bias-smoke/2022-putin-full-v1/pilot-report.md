# Putin 2022 full bias-aware pilot report

## Outcome

The complete eight-chapter researcher, four no-search review rounds, formatter, and
comparative judge flow completed on an isolated catalog. The release is not promoted.
The final score/order audit found that the new bias controls worked, but symmetric
two-record-per-lens compaction dropped decisive Putin evidence and changed several scores
for projection reasons rather than substantive reasons.

## Evidence production

- 60 validated evidence records, 335 mappings, and all 80 coverage rows.
- A cited twelve-part evidence environment with 28 supporting IDs.
- Terminal review covered all eight chapters after three bounded research continuations.
  It reported 6–14 defensible units and 5–10 source families per chapter, retained
  explicit unresolved risks, and requested no further continuation.
- Research, review, and formatting used 4,420,712 tokens with a $1.62–$3.04 equivalent
  cost range. Formatting remained practical at full scope.

## Full failure demonstration

The first eight-chapter review contained all eight substantive bias assessments but
serialized its continuation selection as question IDs plus one newline-joined blob. It
also cited `ENV-02`, while the notebook only contained the composite label `ENV-01/02`.
The original consumer rejected the entire paid review. Tolerant normalization recovered
the eight chapter IDs, removed only the unverifiable citation, and retained a warning.
The exact paid research session resumed without repeating collection.

The comparative judge then returned one copied ruler-year identity and one numeric score
with a null-only review label. Both were deterministically repaired from trusted input
without altering scores or rationales, and all eight judgments validated.

The deeper failure was compaction. Putin 1B retained only four of 26 chapter records and
Putin 8B retained four of 20. Broadly mapped context records were repeatedly selected for
many lenses, displacing direct nuclear-threat, domestic-safety, welfare, and governing-
portfolio evidence. Scores moved old→new as follows: 1B 2→4.5, 2B 1→1, 3B 2→4,
4B 1→1.5, 5B 2→4, 6B 3.5→2.5, 7B 1.5→2, and 8B 6→4.5. Confidence fell sharply in
the projection-limited chapters.

## Audit decision

The independent no-search audit accepted 4B and 7B, requested an attribution-only
rejudge for 2B, retained the old result pending restored evidence for 1B, 3B, and 6B,
and blocked 5B and 8B using the pre-repair run status. Most importantly, it found no
mechanical report-volume penalty or blanket regime correction. It identified selective
compaction—not evidence abundance—as the material distortion.

evidence and deliberately adds distinct records across lenses rather than repeatedly
choosing the same broadly mapped context. Add metamorphic tests proving duplicate or
omnibus context cannot evict decisive evidence. Rebuild the same projections and rerun
only the affected comparative chapters before another no-search audit.
