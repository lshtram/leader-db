## Chapter-by-chapter handoff

### 1B — Nuclear and existential risk

Local baseline:

- No Mexico arsenal or nuclear-use facts were supplied.
- IAEA confirms safeguards under NPT/Tlatelolco and an Additional Protocol (E002).
- Exposure is best characterized as limited nuclear-weapons authority with treaty and dual-use responsibilities; this is not evidence of personal AMLO conduct.

Internet evidence:

- E002 is the only retained direct nuclear item.
- No target-year AMLO nuclear rhetoric, command-safety decision, proliferation decision, or nuclear crisis was found in this pass.

Gaps requiring continuation:

- Mexican civil-nuclear safety and 2022 National Report.
- AMLO-specific positions on nuclear disarmament, dual-use technology, cyber, biological, or AI risk.
- Crisis communication and appointments of technical/security experts.

No defensible ruler-attributed evidence was found for lenses 1B.1–1B.10 beyond formal treaty context.

### 2B — International peace, restraint, and lawful security

Local baseline:

- UCDP records zero supplied state-based and internationalized conflict events/fatalities for 2022 and boundary years (E029).
- SIPRI shows military expenditure increased substantially during the tenure, but this measures spending only (E030).

Internet evidence:

- E003–E005 document Mexico’s 2022 Ukraine diplomacy: opposition to invasion, support for sovereignty/humanitarian access, and proposals for dialogue.
- These are attributable foreign-policy actions, but official rhetoric requires corroboration and does not establish effectiveness.
- No evidence was found that AMLO initiated an external war, supplied weapons, or directed proxy conflict.

Gaps:

- More detailed UN voting records and implementation of Mexico’s proposed mediation initiative.
- Migration/security cooperation with the United States and Central America.
- Arms transfers, military deployments, and accountability for any cross-border operations.
- Direct evidence on truthfulness of threat claims and military procurement motives.

### 3B — Domestic safety, restraint, and protection

Strongest chapter yield.

Local baseline:

- 2022 local indicators show continuing physical-integrity problems, political imprisonment, repression, civil-society pressure, and one-sided violence exposure (E031).
- UCDP one-sided violence records show 51 events and 85 fatalities, but the package explicitly warns that event location does not establish perpetrator or ruler responsibility.
- Local longitudinal signals show adverse civil-liberties/physical-integrity context and increased one-sided violence, with attribution limitations.

Internet evidence:

- E007–E011 directly address militarized public security, National Guard control, impunity, disappearances, torture, excessive force, and complaints.
- E012–E014 provide Ayotzinapa accountability, disappearance, detention, and remedy evidence.
- E017–E018 cover journalist killings and presidential harassment/intimidation of journalists.

Important attribution caveat:

- Cartel violence, state-level policing, and local abuses cannot automatically be attributed to AMLO. Attribution is stronger for the decision to expand military policing, the legal framework, executive rhetoric, and failure to remedy documented abuses.

Unresolved gaps:

- Women, Indigenous communities, migrants, and disability-specific protection.
- Direct evidence on protest policing in 2022 and emergency powers.
- Evidence distinguishing national policy from autonomous state/local conduct.

### 4B — Political freedom versus authoritarian rule

Local baseline:

- E032 records deterioration in V-Dem electoral democracy, liberal democracy, expression, judicial constraints, legislative constraints, and WGI voice/rule-of-law measures.
- Freedom House still describes Mexico as an electoral democracy with regular alternation (E016), an important contrary/baseline point.

Internet evidence:

- E015 documents AMLO-backed Plan B electoral reforms, intended reductions in INE size and supervisory/sanctioning powers, opposition objections, and executive attacks on institutional critics.
- E017–E018 provide direct media-threat and journalist-safety evidence.
- E012–E013 provide mixed institutional evidence: the executive created an Ayotzinapa truth commission and released a public report, while UN observers demanded stronger judicial independence and accountability.

Gaps:

- 2022 treatment of opposition parties, civic organizations, protest movements, and local governments.
- Digital surveillance and administrative harassment.
- Direct evidence on appointment/politicization of courts, audit bodies, and autonomous agencies during the target year.
- Whether electoral reforms were ultimately implemented or blocked; later events should remain retrospective context only.

### 5B — Economic well-being and prosperity

Local baseline:

- E033 supplies 2022 income, GDP, GNI, inequality, consumption, government consumption, capital formation, and longitudinal trajectory.
- Local warnings correctly prevent treating nominal current-dollar increases as real growth.
- Local trends show post-pandemic recovery but weak per-capita PPP trajectory and persistent inequality.

Internet evidence:

- E019–E021 provide independent OECD/IMF assessment: macroeconomic stability and fiscal prudence, but low growth, weak investment/productivity, informality, inequality, inflation, and strained systems.
- E023–E024 document the administration’s stated social/infrastructure priorities and independent evaluation concerns.
- E027–E028 provide audit irregularities and the president’s response to them.

Gaps:

- Specific 2022 appointments and independence of economic professionals.
- Competition, property rights, regulatory changes, and private-investment effects.
- Distribution by region, gender, and household group beyond Gini.
- Detailed implementation and cost-benefit evidence for Tren Maya, Dos Bocas, and AIFA.

### 6B — Social well-being and human development

Local baseline:

- E034 supplies HDI, education, life expectancy, mortality, immunization, literacy, and enrollment outcomes.
- These indicate relatively strong aggregate human-development outcomes but cannot establish AMLO’s contribution or quality/equity.
- E022 and E024 add independent evaluation evidence on social-policy advances, gaps, pandemic effects, and Indigenous/group inequality.

Internet evidence:

- E023 documents the administration’s stated programs and coverage claims.
- E022 is the strongest independent source for social-policy implementation and distributional gaps.
- E009–E011 provide crisis/security harms relevant to avoidable suffering, but should not be double-counted as ordinary social-welfare outcomes.

Gaps:

- Health-system access, affordability, medicine shortages, service quality, water/sanitation, housing, food security, and disability inclusion.
- Political conditionality or benefit distribution by party loyalty.
- Program-level evaluations for pensions, scholarships, Jóvenes Construyendo el Futuro, and Sembrando Vida within the 2022 window.
- Durable institutionalization beyond AMLO’s personal rule.

### 7B — Personal integrity and honesty

Local baseline:

- Country corruption and governance indicators (E035) are context only and cannot establish personal integrity.
- No local fact directly measures AMLO’s truthfulness, personal benefit, family interests, appointments, or response to scrutiny.

Internet evidence:

- E025 documents the 2022 Baker Hughes/Houston-property controversy, AMLO’s request that his son explain finances, and his denial of a conflict.
- E026 is contrary evidence: Baker Hughes reported no irregularity/conflict in its internal review.
- E027–E028 document audit irregularities in flagship projects and AMLO’s public defense of government honesty.
- E018 documents presidential harassment of journalists, potentially relevant to tolerance of scrutiny, but this must be separated from personal self-dealing.

Gaps:

- Asset declarations, procurement/beneficial-ownership records, appointments, campaign finance, and verified family/state-business nexus.
- Independent findings rather than allegations or self-exculpatory statements.
- Evidence of correction, retaliation, concealment, or acceptance of independent investigations.

The personal-nexus record remains mixed and incomplete; generic militarization, corruption, or institutional weakness was not personalized.

### 8B — Effectiveness and competence

Program basis:

- E007 establishes implementation of military reorganization.
- E023–E024 establish social-program implementation claims and independent evaluation concerns.
- E019–E021 provide independent outcome/context evidence.
- E027 documents implementation/audit irregularities in major projects.
- E012–E013 show a mixed implementation/accountability chain in Ayotzinapa: presidential commission and public report, but continuing demands for judicial independence and obstruction remedies.

Gaps:

- Complete program portfolio and prioritization documents for 2022.
- Budgets, appointments, milestones, delivery data, and adaptation/replacement decisions.
- Independent outcomes across more than one major program.
- Crisis management evidence beyond macroeconomic and security outcomes.
- Durability and post-period evidence must be kept retrospective.
