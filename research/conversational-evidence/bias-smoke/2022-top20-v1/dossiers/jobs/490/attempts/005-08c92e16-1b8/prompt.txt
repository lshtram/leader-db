The 2022 research environment was highly constrained: independent media and civil society operated under censorship, surveillance, intimidation, arbitrary detention, and limited safe protest space. Amnesty documented both official records and interviews with victims, lawyers, and former detainees, while noting that the government’s National Human Rights Strategy was prepared without independent civil-society consultation.
