## Chapter-by-chapter baseline and yield

### 1B — Nuclear and existential-risk responsibility

Local package: no relevant facts; all ten lenses explicitly `no_evidence_found`. The package correctly warns that arsenal and operational-capability facts are absent and that nuclear exposure cannot be inferred from generic security data.

Internet yield: E019 only, documenting Nigeria’s formal NPT/IAEA safeguards framework. It is context, not ruler-specific evidence. No target-period evidence was found for Buhari’s nuclear rhetoric, command-and-control decisions, proliferation, arms control, crisis de-escalation, or cyber/biological/AI catastrophic-risk governance.

Status: narrative gap remains across all ten lenses. The local empty state is not evidence of no exposure or responsible conduct.

### 2B — International peace, restraint, and lawful security

Local retained context:

- LF135–LF136: 2022 state-based conflict events and fatalities.
- LF291–LF294: 2022 military expenditure, per-capita spending, GDP share, and government-spending share.
- LS071–LS072: longitudinal conflict-event and conflict-fatality signals.
- Local warnings explicitly prohibit inferring initiation, perpetration, defensive necessity, or ruler responsibility from event/fatality data.

Internet evidence: E009–E013.

The evidence establishes severe domestic/internationalized security exposure, humanitarian need, state military response, and serious allegations concerning security-force conduct. It does not establish that Buhari initiated a war of choice, manipulated threat information, directed unlawful conduct, or pursued/blocked a specific peace settlement in 2022. Military spending is burden/context, not motive.

Source families: UCDP/local structured data, SIPRI/local structured data, UN/OCHA, UNHCR, U.S. State Department, HRW. At least three source families and multiple source types are present.

Internet evidence: E011–E017.

Source families: State House, INEC, Freedom House, HRW, Amnesty, media reporting.
