## Reconnaissance and authority baseline

Buhari was Nigeria’s elected president and head of the federal executive during the target year, with formal responsibility for national policy, federal appointments, security institutions, budgets, and assent to legislation. The 2022 Electoral Act was signed by Buhari on 25 February 2022 after executive–legislative disagreement over provisions. [State House account](https://statehouse.gov.ng/assent-into-law-of-the-electoral-act-2022-landmark-moment-for-the-nation/), [INEC Electoral Act page](https://www.inecnigeria.org/electoral-act-2022/).

Practical constraints included federalism, state-level policing and governance, the National Assembly, independent constitutional bodies, entrenched insecurity, oil dependence, fiscal weakness, inflation, flooding, and external food and energy shocks. Personal direction should not be inferred from country-level outcomes or subordinate conduct without additional evidence.

The information environment was mixed: Nigeria had active civil society, media, courts, international monitors, and human-rights organizations, but journalists and civic actors faced harassment, detention, intimidation, and restrictions. Freedom House described a vibrant media and civil society environment alongside frequent harassment of journalists and obstacles to investigating military abuses. [Freedom House](https://freedomhouse.org/country/nigeria/freedom-world/2022), [NHRC media statement](https://www.nhrc.gov.ng/nhrc-media/news-and-events/381-media-parley-ojukwu-reassures-journalists-of-commitment-to-a-wide-safe-media-space.html). The search pass was English-language only; no local-language archives or Nigerian-language reporting were searched.

Official-statistics and fiscal transparency caveats are material. The IMF noted uncertainty around NNPC financial commitments, tax write-offs, fuel-consumption volumes, and the need for stronger auditing. [IMF 2022 Article IV statement](https://www.imf.org/en/news/articles/2022/11/18/nigeria-staff-concluding-statement-of-the-2022-article-iv-mission).
