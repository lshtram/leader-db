### Chapter 3B — Domestic safety and protection

Local baseline: supplied facts show serious country-level physical-integrity concerns. Accepted evidence:

[HRW COVID section](https://www.hrw.org/world-report/2022/country-chapters/brazil)

### Chapter 2B — International peace/lawful security

New evidence:

- `WEB054`: Brazil’s Foreign Ministry publicly defended the administration’s Ukraine position after Bolsonaro’s Russia visit, stating that Brazil’s positions were clear, public, and repeatedly conveyed to partner governments and the UN Security Council. This is a primary official clarification and should be distinguished from Bolsonaro’s personal public messaging. [Brazilian Foreign Ministry statement](https://www.gov.br/mre/pt-br/canais_atendimento/imprensa/notas-a-imprensa/declaracoes-a-respeito-da-politica-externa-brasileira), lines 334–342.

Contrary searches covered arms transfers, proxy activity, external combat operations, civilian harm, sanctions, and peace initiatives. No defensible target-period evidence of Brazilian proxy warfare, external combat operations, or arms transfers to the Ukraine conflict was located.

The chapter is improved on official diplomatic posture but remains narrow. The Moscow visit should not represent Brazil’s entire international-security conduct.

### Chapter 3B — Domestic safety/protection

New evidence:

- `WEB055`: The Brazilian Health Ministry states that health assistance to the Yanomami deteriorated sharply between 2019 and 2022, producing underreporting of illness and deaths and compromising Indigenous rights. This is a later official retrospective statement; attribution is institutional and tenure-based, not proof of a personal order. [Brazilian Health Ministry FAQ](https://www.gov.br/saude/pt-br/assuntos/missao-yanomami/faq/faq/o-ministerio-da-saude-esconde), lines 297–303.  
  Mappings: `3B.4, 3B.5, 3B.6, 3B.9, 3B.10`

Searches also covered federal remedies, protest policing, surveillance, due process, and complaint channels. No sufficiently precise federal corrective-action or surveillance-oversight record was recovered.

The chapter gains one official retrospective institutional unit, but personal attribution for individual violence and remedy effectiveness remains unresolved.

[TCU Auxílio Brasil audit](https://portal.tcu.gov.br/imprensa/noticias/auditoria-no-auxilio-brasil-constata-desvio-do-publico-alvo-do-programa), lines 6–24.

[TCU 2022 accounts review](https://portal.tcu.gov.br/imprensa/noticias/tcu-aprova-com-ressalvas-as-contas-de-2022-do-ex-presidente-da-republica), lines 40–56, 61–77.
