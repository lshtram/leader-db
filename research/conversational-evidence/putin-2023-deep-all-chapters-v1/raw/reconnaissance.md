## Authority Baseline

- Formal office baseline: the Kremlin describes the President of Russia as head of state, guarantor of the constitution and human/civil rights, with authority over domestic and foreign policy and as commander-in-chief. [Kremlin structure page](https://en.kremlin.ru/structure/president)
- Incumbency baseline: Putin’s official Kremlin bio shows him as president since 2012, with prior presidential and de facto leadership periods. [Kremlin biography](https://en.kremlin.ru/catalog/persons/54/biography)
- Regime baseline: Freedom House says power is concentrated in Putin’s hands, with subservient courts, controlled media, pliable opposition, and no democratic transfer of power. [Freedom House Russia 2024](https://freedomhouse.org/country/russia/freedom-world/2024)
- Domestic legitimacy baseline: Kremlin-adjacent pollsters reported Putin trust in the high 70s during late 2023, but this should be treated as a regime-conditioned indicator rather than neutral public sentiment. [VCIOM, 22 Dec 2023](https://wciom.ru/analytical-reviews/analiticheskii-obzor/reitingi-doverija-politikam-ocenki-raboty-prezidenta-i-pravitelstva-podderzhka-politicheskikh-partii-22122023)

## Major Inherited Conditions Entering 2023

- The full-scale war in Ukraine was already underway, with sanctions, battlefield attrition, and deep diplomatic isolation shaping every 2023 judgment. [Freedom House key developments](https://freedomhouse.org/country/russia/freedom-world/2024)
- Civil liberties were already heavily compressed: opposition channels were constrained, courts were not independent, and the media environment was controlled. [Freedom House overview](https://freedomhouse.org/country/russia/freedom-world/2024)
- Corruption and property-rights insecurity were long-standing structural features, not 2023 novelties. [Transparency International Russia](https://ti-russia.org/en/corruption-perceptions-index/cpi-2023/)
- War mobilization had already begun to distort labor markets, budgets, and industrial allocation before 2023 ended. [Reuters on labor shortage](https://www.investing.com/news/economy/russian-labour-shortage-laid-bare-as-unemployment-drops-to-record-low-3095351)
- Official information access was degraded by wartime secrecy and state opacity, which affects confidence in any internal metrics. [Freedom House](https://freedomhouse.org/country/russia/freedom-world/2024)

## Favorable Evidence

- The Russian economy was more resilient than many expected. The IMF says GDP grew `3.6%` in 2023. [IMF April 2024 WEO briefing](https://www.imf.org/en/news/articles/2024/04/16/tr041624-transcript-of-april-2024-weo-press-briefing)
- The World Bank also described 2023 regional growth as stronger than expected, with Russia and Ukraine returning to growth. [World Bank Europe and Central Asia update](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2024/04/11/sluggish-growth-delaying-economic-recovery-in-emerging-europe-and-central-asia)
- Russia’s official Accounts Chamber said 2023 GDP grew `3.6%`, industrial production rose, and investment hit a record pace. [Kremlin meeting with Accounts Chamber chairman, 23 Jul 2024](https://en.kremlin.ru/events/president/transcripts/copy/74625)
- Reuters reported a tight labour market, strong real-wage gains, and low unemployment, which indicates wartime mobilization did not collapse the labor system. [Reuters via Investing](https://www.investing.com/news/economy/russian-jobless-rate-hits-new-record-low-in-strained-labour-market-3164576?ampMode=1)
- The state kept fiscal and military priorities funded. Reuters reported the 2023 budget came in around `32.2 trillion rubles` and defence spending was extremely elevated. [Reuters via Investing](https://m.uk.investing.com/news/economic-indicators/russian-budget-expenditure-in-2023-to-total-351-billion--finance-minister-3281612?ampMode=1)
- Official polling still showed strong trust and approval for Putin in late 2023. [VCIOM](https://wciom.ru/analytical-reviews/analiticheskii-obzor/reitingi-doverija-politikam-ocenki-raboty-prezidenta-i-pravitelstva-podderzhka-politicheskikh-partii-22122023)

## Adverse Evidence

- International legal and reputational pressure intensified sharply with the ICC warrant. [Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/world-news/icc-judges-issue-arrest-warrant-against-putin-over-alleged-war-crimes-3033607)
- Wagner’s June rebellion exposed elite and coercive fragility. [Freedom House](https://freedomhouse.org/country/russia/freedom-world/2024)
- Navalny’s 19-year sentence and Memorial-related repression show continued systematic anti-opposition enforcement. [RFE/RL Navalny](https://www.rferl.org/a/russia-navalny-sentenced-19-years-/32534422.html), [UN experts on Memorial/Orlov](https://srdefenders.org/russia-search-of-the-homes-offices-of-members-of-the-ngo-memorial-and-criminalisation-of-oleg-orlov-joint-communication/)
- The LGBT extremism ruling signaled further narrowing of civil space and social pluralism. [HRW](https://www.hrw.org/news/2023/11/30/russia-supreme-court-bans-lgbt-movement-extremist)
- Corruption remained severe: Russia scored `26/100` in TI’s 2023 CPI and ranked `141/180`. [Transparency International Russia](https://ti-russia.org/en/corruption-perceptions-index/cpi-2023/)
- Property rights weakened further as the state expanded external management and seizures of foreign-linked assets. [Reuters via Investing](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/factboxmoscow-takes-control-over-assets-of-western-companies-3136331)
- Economic stress was real underneath the headline growth: labour shortages, inflation pressure, and high defence spending all worsened. [Reuters labour shortage](https://www.investing.com/news/economy/russian-labour-shortage-laid-bare-as-unemployment-drops-to-record-low-3095351), [Reuters inflation](https://www.investing.com/news/economy/putin-russian-inflation-could-near-8-this-year-3256747)
