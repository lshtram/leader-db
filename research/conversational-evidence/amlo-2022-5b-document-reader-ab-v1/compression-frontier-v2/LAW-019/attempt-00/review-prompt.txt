## 1. What the regulator says it did in 2022 (factual content, with locators)

### 1.1 Stated strategic reorientation

- The opening message states that CRE in 2022 "ha continuado con la ejecución y consolidación de la estrategia regulatoria acorde a la presente política pública" — i.e., its work was aligned with the **current administration**'s energy policy as set by the federal executive ("las directrices planteadas por el Ejecutivo Federal a través de la actual política energética"). *Unit 1, "Mensaje del Comisionado Presidente".*
- The stated policy objective is **"recuperar la soberanía energética"** and to position the energy sector as **"una palanca ordenada del desarrollo nacional en beneficio de la población"** — explicitly framing the regulator's mission in terms of energy sovereignty and orderly national development, with the population (and "the most vulnerable population") as the stated beneficiary. *Unit 1.*
- The **Plan Estratégico 2022** was **adjusted** ("Se ajustó") to four strategic objectives:
  1. Creation and adjustment of **"Regulación Focalizada"** (focused/targeted regulation) for the Hydrocarbons and Electricity markets;
  2. **Increase of the Commission's revenue** ("incremento de los Ingresos de la Comisión");
  3. **Optimization and improvement of services rendered** ("optimización y mejora de los Servicios prestados");
  4. Creation of an **automated institutional information system** ("creación de un Sistema de Información Institucional automatizado"). *Unit 1.*

**Analytical note (interpretation, not fact):** The strategic plan is presented as a re-purposing of the regulator around the executive's policy direction. Two of the four objectives (revenue, services) are operational/internal; two (focused regulation, information system) are substantive. The phrase "ajustó" implies the 2022 plan was modified from a prior one, but the document does not record the prior version or the diff.

### 1.2 Regulatory output and supervisory action (the only quantified activity in the supplied text)

- **Órgano de Gobierno (Governing Board) of CRE held 35 sessions, issuing 1,983 administrative acts ("actos administrativos")** in 2022. *Unit 2, second paragraph.*

The report states that these visits succeeded in **suspending activities and immobilizing product** ("logrando suspender actividades e inmovilizar producto"). *Unit 2, second paragraph.*

- The report attributes an increase in CRE's revenue from **"derechos, productos y aprovechamientos"** (regulatory fees, asset-yielding products, and other income) to **"el trabajo conjunto de las áreas de la Comisión, junto con la mejora de procesos administrativos"** — i.e., to internal coordination and process improvement. *Unit 2, third paragraph.*

**Analytical note:** The report frames the revenue increase as a positive outcome of compliance with austerity rules — a self-justifying framing. The austerity language is the standard López Obrador-era fiscal frame; the regulator presents itself as observing it.

## 4. Competing accounts, qualifications, and source perspective

The document is **monophonic** — only the regulator speaks. The following qualifications and counter-considerations are **not in the document** but are necessary for the judge to weigh any claim drawn from it:

- **Regulatory-capture risk (5B.5).** The report notes "énfasis en los actores de mayor preponderancia en el sector." For Chapter 5B.5, the consequential question is whether enforcement is symmetric — applied to politically connected **and** non-connected actors — or concentrated where it is safe. The document does not name any actor, firm, or case, so symmetry cannot be assessed from this source alone. Treat the "1,983 actos administrativos" and the "suspender actividades e inmovilizar producto" claims as **volume indicators, not impact indicators**.
- **Independence of the regulator (5B.2).** The report explicitly subordinates the regulator's plan to the executive's directives ("se alinee a las directrices planteadas por el Ejecutivo Federal"). This is a stated fact about **dependence on executive policy**, not a finding of operational independence. The judge should not infer CRE operational autonomy from this document; if anything, the framing supports the opposite reading.
- **Strategic reorientation (5B.4, 5B.1).** The shift from a pre-2022 plan to a 2022 plan with objectives of "Regulación Focalizada" and executive-policy alignment is a stated fact. Whether this represents a move toward **fair competition rules** or toward **discriminatory focused regulation favoring the state productive enterprises (e.g., CFE, Pemex)** is a question the document does not answer — it is silent on CFE/Pemex, on private vs. public permit-holders, on open-access rules, and on the 2013-reform framework. The judge cannot resolve 5B.4 from this document alone.
- **Revenue growth mechanics (5B.5, 5B.7).** The report credits internal efficiency and austerity for revenue gains. Without figures, the alternative explanations — fee schedule changes, enforcement-driven recovery, or volume growth — cannot be excluded. The "cumplimiento normativo de racionalidad y austeridad" phrasing is a signal of compliance with the **Ley de Austeridad Republicana** (2019) regime, not an independent efficiency finding.
- **"Regulación efectivamente aplicada" (5B.7).** This is the report's own success criterion. Whether application is also **fair, transparent, and reviewable** is not addressed. The 1,983 acts volume is presented as evidence of application; the document contains no disposition statistics (confirmed / overturned / appealed), no user-complaint data, and no independent audit reference.
- **Date of issue vs. period covered.** The report is dated 26 April 2023 and covers 2022. This is a ~4-month lag, which is normal for an annual report but means the document was finalized in the early months of the post-2022 political season; the judge should be aware that the report is the regulator's own retrospective, not contemporaneous performance.
- **Geographic/social beneficiary claim.** The document names the **"población más vulnerable"** (Unit 1) and **"los usuarios"** (Unit 2) as beneficiaries.
