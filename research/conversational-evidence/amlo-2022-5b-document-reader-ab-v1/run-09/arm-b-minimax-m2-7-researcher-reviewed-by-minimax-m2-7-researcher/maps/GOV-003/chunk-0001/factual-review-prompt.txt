### Observed limitations and reopeners

1. **Employment metric discrepancy (critical):** 2.4 million total jobs (ENOE) vs 753,000 formal IMSS registrations in the same 2022 period. Document treats these as complementary without acknowledging the distinction. A stronger model should require reconciliation or clarification from SHCP/INEGI.
2. **IEPS fuel subsidy framing:** The 397 billion peso "revenue gain" from lower-than-budgeted IEPS subsidy is presented alongside the 632 billion peso above-programme total, creating an ambiguity — are both figures additive vs offsetting? The document should specify the programme baseline for each.
3. **GDP denominator:** 49.4% debt-to-GDP uses nominal GDP; currency appreciation/depreciation and GDP revisions affect this figure independently of policy. No adjustment or sensitivity stated.
4. **Debt buffer composition:** $262 billion "blindaje" mixes liquid reserves (immediately available) with committed but undrawn facilities (IMF, Fed swap), which are not fungible in a crisis. Composition not disaggregated.
5. **73% debt pressure reduction:** The metric is unexplained — average reduction across which years, which instruments, relative to which baseline? Should be verified against SHCP debt maturity schedule.
6. **Net new borrowing vs refinancing:** Debt operations are presented without net-issuance figures; it is unclear whether 2022 reduced the debt stock or merely extended maturities.
7. **Formal vs informal employment framing:** The 2.4 million job figure is used for headline employment performance while IMSS formal registrations (753,000) are cited for structural quality; both appear without acknowledging the gap.
8. **Revenue attribution:** Increases in ISR and VAT are attributed to enforcement and real-wage growth without isolating inflation effects (which inflate nominal revenue without real increase) or commodity-price base effects on oil revenues.
9. **Sustainability narrative unaccompanied:** The claim of debt sustainability and trajectory improvement is not supported by any model, stress test, or independent assessment in the extract.

Al cierre del cuarto año de la administración, las finanzas públicas continúan con un manejo responsable de los recursos públicos, un nivel estable de la deuda y un ejercicio eficiente del gasto orientado al bienestar de las familias mexicanas.

Al cierre de 2022, se registraron las tasas de desempleo más bajas en 17 años: 2.8% en el total y 2.5% para las mujeres, debido a la creación de 2.4 millones de plazas de trabajo en 2022, en su mayoría formales.
