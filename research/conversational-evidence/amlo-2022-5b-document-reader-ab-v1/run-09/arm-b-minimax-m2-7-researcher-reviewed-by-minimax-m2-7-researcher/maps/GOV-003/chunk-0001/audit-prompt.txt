### Corrected reopeners (previously flagged; now verified or refined)

1. **Employment metric discrepancy (critical):** E3 (2.4 million total occupied population growth, ENOE-based) and E4 (753,000 IMSS formal registrations) are presented without acknowledging the ~1.65 million informal/gap category. The document uses the 2.4 million figure for headline employment performance while citing the 753,000 for structural quality framing — without flagging the distinction. This is an internal inconsistency in the source, not merely a drafting issue.

2. **IEPS subsidy framing (corrected interpretation):** The 397 billion peso figure is explicitly described as "menor recaudación por el estímulo" — a *revenue shortfall* from the fuel subsidy, not a "gain." It offsets rather than adds to the 632 billion above-programme federal revenue. Both figures are real, but they partially cancel each other.

3. **A2 — Growth rate nominal vs real:** The 2.5% revenue growth figure lacks the explicit "real" qualifier found on all other growth rates in this section (18.7%, 7.8%, 11%, etc.). The document context suggests it is real, but this is an omission in the original text, not an explicit specification.

4. **C2 — Deficit arithmetic:** Primary deficit −0.5% + interest 2.9% = public deficit −3.4%. The implied 2.9% of GDP in interest cost appears high relative to the 49.4% debt stock at 4.4% average rate; verify whether RFSP includes non-interest items and whether coverage differences explain the gap.

5. **C4 — Buffer composition:** $262 billion mixes liquid reserves (usable in a crisis) with committed-but-undrawn facilities (IMF Flexible Credit Line, Fed swap) that are not fungible without triggering conditions. Composition not disaggregated.

6. **C7 — 73% debt pressure reduction:** Relative to which baseline? Average of which three years? Across which instruments? Not operationalised in the extract.

7. **D1/D2 vs D5 — Net new borrowing:** Buybacks (68,017m) vs new issuance (48,596m + 12,500m + 50,863m) presented as separate operations; net issuance not computed in the document. Some operations appear to be liability-management swaps reducing refinancing pressure without changing net debt stock.

8. **Revenue attribution:** ISR (+11%) and VAT (+7.8%) attributed to enforcement and real-wage growth; nominal revenue effects from inflation and base effects on oil revenues not isolated.

9. **E9 locator corrected:** Investment growth figures are in Block 3, not Block 2.

10. **D3 — peso formatting:** "7 mil 90 millones" (not 7,090 million) is consistent with Spanish "mil" notation for whole thousands.

En materia fiscal, los ingresos públicos se mantuvieron fuertes. En 2022, sumaron 6 billones 595 mil millones de pesos, ubicándose 422 mil millones de pesos por encima del programa. Respecto a 2021, estos aumentaron en 2.5% y ligaron dos años de crecimientos consecutivos.
En 2022, el Gobierno Federal obtuvo ingresos adicionales al programa en 632 mil millones de pesos, de los cuales 397 mil millones de pesos correspondieron a la menor recaudación por el estímulo al IEPS de combustibles.
Impulsados por los altos precios del petróleo, los ingresos petroleros ascendieron a 1 billón 482 mil millones de pesos, equivalente a 5.2% del PIB, el nivel más alto observado desde 2014. Con respecto a 2021, se registró un incremento real anual de 18.7%.
