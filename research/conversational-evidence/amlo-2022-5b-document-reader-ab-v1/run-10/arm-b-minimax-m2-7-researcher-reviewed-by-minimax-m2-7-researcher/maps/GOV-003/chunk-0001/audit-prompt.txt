## §7 — Limitations and Source-Role Flags

1. **Boundary ambiguity:** "Sector público," "gobierno federal," and "balance público" are used interchangeably without clarifying whether figures refer to central government, general government, or public sector (including PEMEX/CFE quasi-fiscal activity).
2. **Nominal vs. real:** Many growth figures are stated as "real" but the deflator (INPC, implicit GDP deflator) is not identified.
3. **Budget vs. actuals:** The "programmed" baseline is not tied to a specific approved budget version; mid-year re-estimates are not discussed.
4. **Audited vs. preliminary:** No distinction is made between audited results and preliminary close figures.
5. **Contingent liabilities:** Arrears, guarantees, off-budget state enterprises (PEMEX/CFE deficits) are not quantified, though RFSP at 4.4% suggests they are partially captured.
6. **Debt definition:** Gross vs. net debt not specified; domestic vs. external split not provided in extract.
7. **Self-reportage:** As an executive-official press release, all claims are self-attributed; no independent audit institution (ASF — Auditoría Superior de la Federación) is cited.
8. **Causality language:** Phrases like "este resultado fue posible por…" attribute outcomes to policy when external factors (oil prices, US demand, remittances) are dominant drivers.
9. **Forward-looking statements:** Projections for 2023 growth and debt pressures are presented without probability ranges or scenario analysis.
10. **Cross-currency:** Some comparisons use USD, some MXN; exchange-rate assumptions are not stated.

## REOPEN REQUESTS

1. **Internal inconsistency — unemployment rate:** Block 2 (¶5) reports unemployment rates of 2.8% national and 2.5% for women ("las tasas de desempleo más bajas en 17 años"), while Block 3 (¶10) reports the 2022 *average* unemployment rate was 3.3% ("la tasa de desempleo promedio del año fue de las más bajas desde que se tiene registro"). These are not directly contradictory (point-in-time vs. annual average, different definitions), but the source map should flag this as requiring clarification. The extract does not reconcile the two figures.

2. **Internal inconsistency — employment scope:** Block 2 (¶5) states "2.4 millones de plazas de trabajo en 2022, en su mayoría formales" while Block 3 (¶10) specifies "753 mil nuevas plazas ante el IMSS... principalmente de carácter permanente." The relationship between the broader 2.4 million figure and the narrower 753,000 IMSS-registered figure is unexplained in the extract.

3. **Internal inconsistency — FDI figures:** Block 3 (¶7) reports total IED of "32 mil millones de dólares" while the same block at ¶12 reports "nuevas inversiones registradas... de 14 mil millones de dólares." The extract does not explain what portion of the $32B total flows represents new investment vs. reinvested earnings or inter-company loans, nor why new investments are reported as Q3 partial-year figures while total IED is also Q3.

4. **Block 5 locator correction — debt sustainability claim:** The claim "Debt is sustainable and lower than 2021" was mapped to "Block 5, ¶14" in the draft but the text appears at Block 5, ¶20. The paragraph numbering within Block 5 in the draft does not correspond to the extracted block structure; the corrected locator is Block 5, ¶20.
