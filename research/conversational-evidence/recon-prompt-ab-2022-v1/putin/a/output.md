Vladimir Putin was Russia’s serving president and commander-in-chief throughout 2022. Formal constitutional authority, the official record of his addresses and decrees, and specialist analysis all indicate that he personally dominated strategic policy. The invasion was unusually centralized around him, while Parliament, the Security Council, technocrats, security agencies, and military capacity constrained implementation more than strategic choice.

The evidence environment deteriorated sharply after February 2022. Wartime censorship, blocked independent outlets, journalist exile, propaganda, self-censorship, and criminal penalties make domestic approval, elite opinion, casualty, protest, and policy-performance claims unusually difficult to interpret. Official Russian records remain strong evidence of acts and stated policy, but not of their legality, results, public acceptance, or underlying motives.
