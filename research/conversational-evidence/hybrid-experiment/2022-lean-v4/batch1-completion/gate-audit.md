## Quality decision

- Xi: seven chapters passed. Chapter 7B retained a credible gap because direct
  personal-integrity evidence was not strong enough; no quota-padding follow-up ran.
- Scholz: six chapters passed directly. One bounded follow-up added six claims for 3B
  and 7B. Final review retained a credible 3B gap and manual review for 7B, where the
  personal nexus remained limited.
- Both dossiers contain explicit contrary evidence, ruler-attribution limits, and
  source-family warnings. These limitations will reduce judgment confidence rather
  than mechanically change scores.

Decision: pass the evidence flow to the next five-ruler batch. Comparative judgment
remains deferred until the full 20-ruler evidence cohort is assembled, so every chapter
uses one common meter.
