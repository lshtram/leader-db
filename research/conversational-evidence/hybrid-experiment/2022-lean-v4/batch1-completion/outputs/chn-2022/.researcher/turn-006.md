**Search and Rejection Summary**
I reviewed roughly 30 documents across official Chinese policy releases, NBS statistical releases, the PBOC, the IMF, the World Bank, Reuters, and OECD-style structural commentary. I kept 16 non-duplicative URLs that collectively cover macro stabilization, monetary easing, housing rescue, opening-up, small-business support, data-policy design, COVID policy correction, leadership composition, household outcomes, R&D, and anti-corruption.

I rejected or de-prioritized:
- Duplicate Reuters syndications and mirrors of the same underlying story, especially on the leadership reshuffle, property rescue, and COVID rollback.
- Out-of-period follow-ups from 2023-2025 that were useful only as retrospectives but did not add target-year 2022 ruler-period detail.
- Broad China-economy commentary without a clear 2022 ruler-attributed policy claim.
- Background pieces on SOEs, competition, or productivity that repeated the same structural critique already covered by the IMF and World Bank.
- Structured dataset re-fetches already covered by the local priors, including WDI-style growth, income, and spending series.

**Remaining Gaps**
- Direct evidence of Xi personally choosing specific macro instruments is still limited; many concrete 2022 measures are formally State Council, PBOC, NDRC, or regulator actions within a Xi-led system.
- Distributional evidence is still mostly household averages and rural-vs-urban splits; I do not yet have strong independent evidence on wealth concentration, Gini movement, or regional divergence beyond those proxies.
- The evidence on appointments is strong for loyalist consolidation, but weaker on whether any economic technocrats retained meaningful autonomy inside the 2022 system.
- Anti-corruption and favoritism evidence is still mostly official self-reporting; more independent work on finance, SOEs, and local patronage would help.
- If you want, the next useful search would be on independent business-confidence surveys, FDI flows, and 2022 property-sector distress transmission to households and local government finance.
