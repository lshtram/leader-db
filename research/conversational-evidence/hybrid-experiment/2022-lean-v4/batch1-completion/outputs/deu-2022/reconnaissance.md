**Office and constraints**
Olaf Scholz was Germany’s Federal Chancellor in 2022, elected on **8 December 2021** and leading the first federal SPD-Greens-FDP coalition. Under the Basic Law, the chancellor sets policy guidelines and bears overall responsibility, but the job is constrained by ministerial autonomy, coalition bargaining, Bundestag majority rules, Bundesrat participation in many laws, and the constitutional debt brake.

The most important 2022 constraints on Scholz were:
- coalition dependence on SPD, Greens, and FDP
- parliamentary approval for major spending and constitutional changes
- federalism and Bundesrat veto points
- the need for a two-thirds majority for the Bundeswehr special fund’s constitutional amendment
- the war in Ukraine and the energy shock, which narrowed policy space quickly

**Major 2022 events and inherited conditions**
- He entered 2022 with a new three-party coalition and a reform-heavy coalition agreement.
- Russia’s invasion of Ukraine on **24 February 2022** forced a major policy pivot.
- Scholz’s **27 February 2022** Bundestag statement introduced the “Zeitenwende” framing.
- The government pushed a **100 billion euro** Bundeswehr special fund in spring/summer 2022.
- By late summer and autumn, the war had become an energy and inflation crisis.
- On **29 September 2022**, the government announced a **200 billion euro** economic shield / gas price brake package.
- Through 2022, Scholz faced criticism for cautious leadership, especially on Ukraine and energy decisions.

**Compact event map**
- **8 Dec 2021**: Scholz takes office, leading the SPD-Greens-FDP coalition.
- **24 Feb 2022**: Russia invades Ukraine; Germany’s security and energy assumptions break.
- **27 Feb 2022**: Scholz announces the “Zeitenwende,” defense rearmament, energy diversification, and allied commitments.
- **Spring 2022**: Major public and coalition debate over weapons, nuclear sharing, and dependence on Russian energy.
- **3 Jun / 22 Jun 2022**: Bundestag and then government steps formalize the Bundeswehr special fund.
- **Late summer 2022**: Energy costs and inflation dominate domestic politics; coalition friction rises.
- **29 Sep 2022**: Government announces the 200 billion euro economic shield and gas price brake.
- **End 2022**: Scholz is still in office, but his style is widely read as cautious and crisis-driven rather than transformative.
