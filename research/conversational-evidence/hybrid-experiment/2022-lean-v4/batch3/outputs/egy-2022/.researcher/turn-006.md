Remaining gaps:
- The biggest unresolved issue is implementation quality: there is clear evidence of announced reforms, but weaker evidence on how fully they were carried out inside 2022.
- Appointment evidence is thin beyond the CBE governor change; I did not find equally strong 2022 evidence on broader economic-team quality or delegation.
- Household distribution data are better than in many cases, but region-by-region and class-by-class impacts are still only partially visible.
- The military/state-enterprise footprint is clearly important, but exact 2022 profit extraction, privileges, and divestment progress remain opaque.
- More search could refine the separation between crisis response and intentional reform, but it is unlikely to change the broad reading: 2022 under Sisi was a crisis-management year with genuine stabilization moves, some pro-private-sector signaling, and major inflation, devaluation, and distributional stress.
