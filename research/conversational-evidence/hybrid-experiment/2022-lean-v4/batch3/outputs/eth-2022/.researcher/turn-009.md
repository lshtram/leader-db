- I kept sources that either stated Abiy's 2022 program directly, showed concrete implementation actions, or gave credible outcome evidence tied to the reform agenda.
- I rejected or downgraded items that were too generic, too far removed from ruler-level execution, or too easy to personalize from country context alone: broad governance indicators, generic conflict/humanitarian descriptions, and documents that showed capacity or need but not ruler-owned action.
- I excluded several 2024 and 2026 retrospective pieces even when useful as background, because the 2022-specific record was already supported by direct 2022 evidence.
- I treated some mirrored Reuters items on syndication pages as one canonical Reuters claim each, rather than padding the dossier with duplicate publishers.
- A few IMF eLibrary pages were blocked at the source level; where relevant I used the accessible IMF press release and search-result summaries instead.

- The clearest gap is direct internal evidence of how Abiy himself managed implementation bottlenecks inside ministries, regions, and SOEs beyond public speeches and delegated appointments.
- I still do not have a clean, independent documentary trail for the full Homegrown Economic Reform Agenda by end-2022, especially on what was implemented versus merely announced.
- The SOE and telecom reforms are real and concrete, but attribution is partly indirect: technocrats and state entities did much of the operational work.
