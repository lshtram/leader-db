I rejected or downgraded:
- 2023-2025 follow-ups that were outside the target year unless they clearly served as retrospective evidence about a 2022 act.
- Broad misinformation or press-freedom pieces that did not tie tightly enough to Marcos Jr.'s own statements, appointments, or use of office.
- Duplicate syndications of the same story.
- Articles that mainly described the Marcos family, the broader political environment, or inherited corruption without a usable ruler-specific nexus.
- Items that were too general to separate personal integrity from ordinary political propaganda.

Blocked or technically awkward pages:
- LawPhil and some Inquirer/SCMP pages were not cleanly openable in-browser, so I relied on the accessible search-result text or alternate accessible mirrors when the claim was independently corroborated.
