- I rejected several duplicate campaign-disinformation items when they repeated the same Marcos/media issue already covered better by the Reuters Institute, Carter Center, or CHR.
- I excluded later 2024-2026 material unless it was a retrospective report about the 2022 period, as with ACLED or the Carter Center final report.
