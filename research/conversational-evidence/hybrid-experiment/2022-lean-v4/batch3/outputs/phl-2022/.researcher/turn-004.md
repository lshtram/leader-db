- I kept the clearest ruler-attributed 2022 conduct on human-rights policy, drug-war framing, protest handling, red-tagging, and the Percy Lapid response.
- I rejected repeated or overlapping press-freedom summaries from Reuters Institute and RSF when they mostly repeated the same red-tagging/press-risk story already covered by CPJ, HRW, and the U.S. State report.
- I dropped older baseline material from 2020-2021 unless it was needed as inherited context, because the chapter is about 2022 conduct.
- I excluded general election-controversy coverage, foreign-policy pieces, and economic reporting that did not bear directly on domestic physical safety.
- I also downranked claims that were purely official self-description when independent reporting already captured the same issue more directly.

**Remaining gaps**
- The weakest part is direct chain-of-command evidence: I do not yet have a clean 2022 source showing Marcos personally ordered police, intelligence, or NTF-ELCAC actions that produced or stopped red-tagging.
- There is still limited hard evidence on whether the proclaimed anti-drug shift materially reduced abuse beyond official framing and a few reactive investigations.
- More search could help on prison conditions, anti-terror enforcement, and provincial protest policing, but it is unlikely to change the core picture: recurring rights abuses, continued red-tagging, and some reactive remedial steps rather than a clearly transformed safety environment.
