**Office, Authority, Constraints**
- Marcos was proclaimed president-elect on **May 25, 2022** and sworn in on **June 30, 2022**. That makes **June 30, 2022** the start of the ruler period for 2022.
- His office is the Philippine presidency. Under **Article VII of the 1987 Constitution**, executive power is vested in the President, the term begins at **noon on June 30**, and the President has control over executive departments and offices.
- A concrete example of that authority is **Executive Order No. 1** on **June 30, 2022**, which reorganized the Office of the President and abolished the Presidential Anti-Corruption Commission and the Office of the Cabinet Secretary.
- The main constraints are structural: Congress, the courts, the budget process, local governments, treaty obligations, and the inherited economy/security environment. The local prior already covers the country-year indicators for rights, corruption, governance, conflict, and welfare, so I did not re-fetch those structured datasets.

**Inherited Conditions / Major 2022 Context**
- Post-pandemic recovery was incomplete.
- Inflation and joblessness were immediate economic priorities.
- The Duterte-era human-rights and press-freedom controversies remained live issues.
- The Philippines also faced South China Sea tensions and repeated disaster exposure, especially typhoons.

- **May 25, 2022**: Congress proclaims Marcos president-elect.

- **July 25, 2022**: First SONA sets the agenda around recovery, COVID, education, infrastructure, and sovereignty.
