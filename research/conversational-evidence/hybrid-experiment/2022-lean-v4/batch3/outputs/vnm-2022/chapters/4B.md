Rejected or not retained:
- Re-fetches of structured datasets already supplied locally, including Freedom House, V-Dem, RSF, and similar country-year metrics.
- Duplicate syndications of the same Reuters story when they added no new materially distinct claim.
- Out-of-period pieces that only repeated the same 2022 pattern without improving attribution or specificity.
- Pages whose accessible text was too noisy or redundant to add a distinct claim beyond the retained HRW/State Dept/Reuters material.
- Broad background pages on Vietnam that did not bear on political freedom, media control, or succession in a ruler-attributable way.

**Remaining gaps**
- Direct evidence that Nguyễn Phú Trọng personally ordered specific arrests, censorship actions, or electoral manipulations in 2022 remains limited. Most evidence is institutional or state-level, attributed to the CPV-state under his leadership rather than to a signed personal directive.
- More local-language party or ministry directives could tighten attribution, especially for digital controls and succession rules.
- Additional favorable evidence is thin. The main counterweight is formal legal language promising press freedom and no prior censorship, plus occasional corrective action noted in the State Department report, but that does not materially offset the 2022 practice pattern.
