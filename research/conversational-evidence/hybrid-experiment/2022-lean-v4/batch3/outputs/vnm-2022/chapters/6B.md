I rejected or de-prioritized:
- Global or non-Viet Nam-only reports that were too generic for ruler-period attribution.
- Older background pages when a sharper 2022 source existed for the same claim.
- Sources focused mainly on macroeconomics or business conditions unless they had a direct social-welfare channel.
- Duplicate summaries of the same UNICEF/World Bank claims.
- 2025 retrospective items where the 2022-specific pages already supplied cleaner evidence.
- Media-only pieces when an official, UNICEF, WHO, or World Bank source covered the same point better.

**Remaining gaps**
- Direct evidence on Trọng's personal role in social-service administration is still thin. Most evidence is system-level and party-state level, not a documented one-person instruction chain.
- National implementation data on social protection reach, benefit adequacy, and subgroup coverage in 2022 is still incomplete, especially for adults outside child-focused programs.
- More work would help on whether 2022 recovery and social-policy measures materially improved outcomes for the poorest quintile, ethnic minorities, and informal workers by year end rather than only on paper.
