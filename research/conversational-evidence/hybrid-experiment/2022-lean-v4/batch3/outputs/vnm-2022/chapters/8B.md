**Search and rejection summary**
I screened roughly 25-40 documents across Government News, the national government portal, the National Statistics Office, Reuters syndications, the IMF/World Bank trail, VietnamPlus/VietnamNews, and a few local-language pages. I kept the sources that best showed a chain from stated program to budgets, coordination, implementation, outcomes, and bottlenecks.

Rejected or de-prioritized:
- Generic macro pages that repeated the same GDP or inflation figures without adding ruler-specific implementation context.
- Duplicate reprints of the same anti-corruption meeting or the same Reuters wire story on multiple syndication sites.
- Purely moral, rights, or corruption-trend material when it did not help the 8B execution chain.
- Out-of-period retrospectives and 2024-2026 follow-ups that were not needed to reconstruct 2022 execution.
- Blocked or timeout pages when an official snippet or alternate source already gave the same claim.
- Pages that only showed Trọng's title or office without a program, implementation, or outcome link.

**Remaining gaps**
- The strongest evidence is still mostly program-and-institution level. There is less direct proof of Trọng personally managing day-to-day implementation beyond chairing the central committee and setting strategic direction.
- Foreign-policy execution is better evidenced than domestic administrative execution, but even there the record is mostly coordination and commitments rather than outcome audits.
- More search would help if you want sharper evidence on whether the 2022 recovery package and anti-corruption drive materially changed subnational delivery, not just central targets and aggregate outcomes.
- I did not find a clean, ruler-specific audit of personnel replacements or a detailed before/after comparison showing which implementers were promoted or removed because of performance.
