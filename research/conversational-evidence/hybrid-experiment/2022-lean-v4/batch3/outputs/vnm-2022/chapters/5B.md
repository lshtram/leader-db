**Search and rejection summary**
I considered roughly 30 documents across macro policy, fiscal/monetary recovery, poverty and labor, investment, and anti-corruption effects. I retained 13 distinct URLs and 17 source-claim units.

Kept as central evidence:
- IMF 2022 Article IV report and press release
- World Bank January and August 2022 Vietnam outlook updates
- Government resolution and implementation directive on the socio-economic recovery program
- MPI year-end 2022 socio-economic and FDI reports
- World Bank poverty and equity assessment
- ILO labor and social trends report
- Reuters reporting on anti-graft economic friction, market stress, and liquidity pressure
- SBV explanation of 2022 interest-rate tightening

Rejected or not retained:
- Duplicate syndications of the same Reuters stories when they added no new materially distinct claim
- Generic macro pages that repeated the same 2022 growth figures without adding ruler-relevant attribution, distribution, or implementation detail
- Sources that were mostly 2023 or later retrospectives unless they directly clarified a 2022 policy move
- Broad background pieces on Vietnam's economy that did not add anything beyond the IMF, World Bank, MPI, or SBV materials
- Local structured priors were not re-fetched, per the instructions already supplied in the thread

**Remaining gaps**
- Direct ruler-attributed evidence for Nguyễn Phú Trọng personally choosing specific economic appointments in 2022 is weak. Most usable evidence is system-level or government/SBV-level, not a signed personal directive.
- Household-level 2022 evidence is thinner than aggregate macro evidence. More direct 2022 microdata on wages, median incomes, regional poverty, and distribution would help.
- The anti-graft campaign cuts both ways: it likely targeted corruption, but the available evidence also shows real bottlenecks and investor chill. More search on net welfare effects would help.
- More evidence on implementation quality for the recovery package, especially public investment disbursement and project selection, would tighten 5B.6 and 5B.7.
