**Search and Rejection Summary**
I reviewed roughly 30 documents across IBGE releases, Banco Central reports and Copom materials, IMF Article IV material, Planalto constitutional and legal texts, Ministry of Cidadania implementation notices, Reuters/UOL reporting, and a smaller number of broader Brazil-economy explainers.

Retained because they added distinct evidence:
- IMF and BCB materials for macro stability, inflation, and debt.
- IBGE labor, income, inequality, and poverty releases for household welfare and distribution.
- EC 123 and Auxílio Brasil notices for direct policy action on transfers and fuel relief.
- Reuters/UOL reporting for appointment choices, spending-cap intentions, and election-year fiscal priorities.

Rejected or de-prioritized:
- Duplicate Reuters mirrors of the same story once one version was kept.
- Generic 2022 Brazil economy explainers that repeated the same macro facts without new attribution.
- Broad commodity-price or global-inflation commentary that did not change the ruler-specific analysis.
- Post-2023 retrospectives that did not add a new 2022 fact beyond the IBGE/IMF releases already kept.
- Sources focused on political or electoral conflict rather than economic well-being.

**Remaining Gaps**
- Direct evidence on Bolsonaro's own competence as an economic manager is still mixed because many 2022 outcomes came from the labor-market rebound, commodity revenues, and the independent central bank rather than from presidential control alone.
- The strongest ruler-specific positives are the 2022 transfer package, the Auxílio Brasil expansion, and appointment/staffing choices. The strongest negatives are fiscal looseness, election-year spending priorities, and the willingness to loosen the spending cap.
- I still have only partial evidence on whether economic gains were distributed fairly across genders, racial groups, and regions once social transfers are separated from labor-market recovery.
- More search would help on 2022 tax expenditure beneficiaries, public-investment execution, and whether Bolsonaro empowered or sidelined technocrats in the finance and planning apparatus.
