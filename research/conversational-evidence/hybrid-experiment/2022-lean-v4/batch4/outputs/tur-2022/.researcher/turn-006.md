Search and rejection summary
- I considered roughly 25-40 documents across IMF, World Bank, OECD, Reuters mirrors, Freedom House, Transparency International, and Turkish/economic policy material.
- I rejected multiple near-duplicate Reuters items that repeated the same low-rate / inflation / lira story without adding a new materially distinct claim.
- I rejected broader 2025-2026 World Bank country pages as too retrospective for a 2022 ruler-period dossier.
- I rejected general governance or climate pages when they did not add ruler-specific 2022 economic choice, implementation, or attributable outcome.
- I also skipped inaccessible or erroring pages when the Reuters text was already available through a usable mirror.
- I did not use local structured datasets as standalone evidence; the local priors were treated as context only.

Remaining gaps
- Direct 2022 evidence on how Erdoğan weighed inflation against household welfare is still thin beyond IMF/OECD/World Bank analysis.
- I did not find a clean 2022 audited source on regional distribution, firm-level capture, or household welfare that links more directly to Erdoğan's own instructions.
- The best evidence on productivity foundations is partly prospective and climate-oriented rather than a direct audit of 2022 education, health, technology, or administrative-capacity spending.
- More search would help if you want a stronger 5B record on fiscal execution, procurement audits, or the exact role of Erdoğan versus the CBRT and cabinet in each 2022 decision.
