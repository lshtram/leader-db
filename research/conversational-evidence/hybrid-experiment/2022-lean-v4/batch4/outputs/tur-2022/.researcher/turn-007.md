- I avoided reusing the same passage through multiple mirrors unless the mirror added a materially distinct claim or a better locator.

**Remaining Gaps**
- The strongest evidence is on health coverage, refugee-access systems, and child cash-transfer programs; I did not find equally strong 2022 evidence for housing, sanitation, or food-security policy that was both ruler-specific and not redundant.
- Distributional evidence is better for refugees, children, and low-income households than for women, disabled people, or rural regions specifically.
- The record still leans on donor-UNICEF/WHO partnerships in several places, so Erdoğan attribution is meaningful but not exclusive.
- More search would help if you want tighter evidence on direct Turkish-budget social spending, school-quality outcomes, or how much of the welfare architecture in 2022 was Erdoğan-directed versus inherited system maintenance.
