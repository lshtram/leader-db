**Search and rejection summary**
- I reviewed roughly 25-40 documents across the European Commission, OSCE/ODIHR, Venice Commission summaries, Freedom House, RSF, Reuters Institute, Reuters syndications, HRW, Amnesty, CPJ, and Turkish official/legal material.
- I retained the strongest non-duplicative items on four mechanisms: electoral-law manipulation, parliamentary/judicial weakening, media capture, and direct opposition/press suppression.
- I did not retain additional press-freedom or opposition articles when they repeated the same event without adding a materially different claim.
- I considered Freedom House 2022/2023 Turkey pages and the OSCE/ODIHR joint-opinion pages, but the European Commission report already captured the key 2022 electoral and institutional findings in a more directly citable form.
- I excluded 2023 election-observation material as out-of-period for the target year, except where later material explicitly summarized 2022 conditions.
- I also excluded broad current RSF country pages as too temporally distant when a 2022-anchored source was available.
- The picture from the retained sources is consistent: 2022 did not show liberalization; it showed continued institutional centralization, tighter media control, and selective pressure on rivals.

**Remaining gaps**
- I still do not have a 2022 source showing a direct presidential order on the contested cases; attribution is mostly institutional and systemic, not command-level for every incident.
- The term-limit / transfer question remains mostly structural in 2022: the constitution and presidential-system design matter, but there was no direct 2022 succession test.
- More Turkish-language reporting on the March 2022 electoral-law process would help sharpen how much of the rule change was deliberate entrenchment versus routine legislative adjustment.
- More primary documentation on RTÜK, the Press Card Commission, and election-board appointments would tighten 4B.6 and 4B.9, but the current record is already sufficient to show a restrictive political-freedom environment.
