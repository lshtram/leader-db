**Search and Rejection Summary**
I reviewed roughly 30 documents across SBB planning pages, Invest.gov summaries, CBRT releases, IMF and World Bank reports, TÜİK data-portal pages, UN and Reuters/NATO diplomacy coverage, and several Reuters mirrors or syndication pages. I kept sources that directly established the 2022 program, concrete allocations, implementation, measurable outcomes, or ruler-linked diplomacy. I rejected duplicate mirror versions of the same Reuters stories, general governance or rights articles that did not establish execution of Erdoğan's 2022 program, and JS-gated statistics pages unless the search snippet supplied the needed official numbers. Some 2023 retrospective reports were used only because they reported 2022 outcomes.

**Remaining Gaps**
- No internal memo, cabinet minute, or presidential instruction shows Erdoğan personally authored every priority or monitored execution step.
- Project-level completion evidence is thin; the investment program shows allocation and priority, not audited completion.
- Macro outcomes are still confounded by the war in Ukraine, energy and food shocks, the post-pandemic rebound, tourism, and the inherited currency/inflation problem.
- More search would help on audited project completion, direct presidential speeches about 2022 implementation milestones, and independent evaluation of the FX-protected deposit scheme and other emergency measures.
