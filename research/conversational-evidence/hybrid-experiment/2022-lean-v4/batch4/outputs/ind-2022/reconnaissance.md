**Office, Authority, Constraints**
- Modi was India’s Prime Minister in 2022, in his second term that ran from 2019 to 2024 according to the PMO biography. The office is constitutionally the head of government, not an unconstrained executive.
- Under [Article 74](https://www.constitutionofindia.net/articles/article-74-council-of-ministers-to-aid-and-advise-president/) and [Article 75](https://www.constitutionofindia.net/articles/article-75-other-provisions-as-to-ministers/), the PM heads the Council of Ministers, the President acts on ministerial advice, ministers are appointed on the PM’s advice, and the Council is collectively responsible to the Lok Sabha.
- Practical constraints in 2022 were federal and institutional: state and union-territory governments retained primary responsibility for law and order, and rights/media/judicial constraints remained active in the background.
