**Search and rejection summary**
I reviewed roughly 30 documents across Reuters wires and mirrors, Treasury sanctions notices, VOA, Al Jazeera, Euronews, and older Reuters investigations on Setad. I kept the highest-value non-duplicative items and rejected several groups:
- Duplicate protest articles that repeated the same Khamenei remarks without adding new claims.
- Purely 3B/4B items about repression, arrests, and internet shutdowns when they did not add a personal-integrity nexus.
- Later 2023 schoolgirl-poisoning stories, which were outside the 2022 target period.
- Generic country corruption indicators and inherited authoritarian context that do not by themselves prove Khamenei’s personal dishonesty.
- Repeated Reuters mirror copies of the same wire when the underlying claim was already captured once.
- Broad commentary on Iran’s elite politics that lacked a ruler-specific honesty, conflict-of-interest, or concealment claim.

**Remaining gaps**
- Direct 2022 evidence of Khamenei’s own assets, gifts, or personal financial benefit is still thin. The Setad material shows control and opaque power, but not personal enrichment.
- There is little direct evidence of him admitting error or correcting false claims in 2022.
- More target-year material on appointments, family access, and any documented intervention in procurement, licensing, or enforcement would help 7B.3 to 7B.6.
- A tighter 2022 trail from Khamenei’s office to specific censorship or investigative obstruction decisions would strengthen the 7B.7 attribution.
- If you want a stronger integrity dossier, the next useful search would be on official transcripts of Khamenei’s 2022 speeches, sanctions findings about his office-linked entities, and investigative reporting on Mojtaba Khamenei or other office-linked intermediaries in 2022.
