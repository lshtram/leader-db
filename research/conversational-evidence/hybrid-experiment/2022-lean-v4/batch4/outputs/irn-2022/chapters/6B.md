**Search and rejection summary**
I reviewed roughly 30 documents across WHO, UNICEF Iran, UNICEF DATA, World Bank, UNESCO, and Reuters-adjacent coverage, then kept 12 unique URLs plus the reused items above. I rejected or deprioritized several groups:
- Duplicate UNICEF country pages in English and Persian that repeated the same social-protection, education, or WASH language without adding a new claim.
- Generic global UNICEF or UNESCO reports that did not add Iran-specific 2022 evidence.
- Later 2023-2026 UNICEF pages that were useful only as background unless they explicitly referenced 2022 data or 2021-2022 surveys.
- Protest, detention, and censorship sources that belong primarily in 3B or 4B rather than 6B.
- Pure macroeconomic or sanctions commentary that was better left to 5B unless it had a direct welfare linkage such as food prices, nutrition, or social protection.
- A few candidate education/screening items were dropped because a tighter 2022 analogue existed, such as the Afghan-refugee health-screening story and the measles-campaign story.

**Remaining gaps**
- The dossier still lacks direct evidence that Ali Khamenei personally ordered, designed, or corrected social-welfare policy in 2022 rather than presiding over a regime-level ministry/UNICEF delivery structure.
- Household-level distribution data are thin: I have broad WASH, vaccination, nutrition, and social-protection indicators, but less direct evidence on regional inequality, informal settlement coverage, or whether the poorest households actually benefited more than the national averages suggest.
- There is still little independent evaluation of service quality and durability for 2022 social programs, especially education outcomes, nutrition surveillance, and social-protection targeting.
- More search would help if you want a tighter ruler-attribution chain from Khamenei to budget priorities, ministry appointments, or signed policy directives, or if you want independent household survey evidence on who benefited and who was left out.
