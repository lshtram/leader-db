Search and rejection summary

I screened roughly 30 documents across IMF, World Bank, Reuters, Reuters Institute, Freedom House, Transparency International, the Thai Ministry of Finance, the Thai government PRD, Bangkok Post, Nation Thailand, and Reuters syndication mirrors.

Kept because they added distinct, non-duplicative 2022 claims:
- IMF June 2022 mission statement for policy design, targeted support, debt restructuring, and productivity investment.
- IMF September 2022 Article IV press release for inflation, fiscal consolidation, governance, and household-debt response.
- World Bank December 2022 press release for household-welfare mitigation and poverty trajectory.
- World Bank October 2022 rural diagnostic for distributional concentration, rural income loss, and rural productivity/connectivity needs.
- Nation Thailand / Reuters budget reporting for the 2023 fiscal budget debate in 2022.
- Reuters reporting on diesel tax cuts, low-income targeting, and household debt as a 2022 shock-management package.
- Freedom House and Transparency International for corruption, cronyism, and public-sector integrity.
- PRD award article as retrospective corroboration of the finance minister's 2022-era economic management.

Rejected or deprioritized:
- Reuters Connect image-only records and similar pages with no substantive economic claim.
- Out-of-period 2023-2026 pages that did not add target-year evidence.
- Duplicate Reuters rewrites of the same budget, fuel-price, or debt stories when a cleaner 2022 source already existed.
- General country macro pages that repeated the local structured priors without adding ruler-period attribution.
- Sources that were inaccessible or erroring when a better contemporaneous replacement existed.
- Articles focused on opposition politics or media freedom that were not materially economic, even if they mentioned Prayut.
