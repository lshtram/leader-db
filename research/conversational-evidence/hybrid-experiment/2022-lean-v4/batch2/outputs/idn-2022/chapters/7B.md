**Search and rejection summary**
- I reviewed roughly 25-35 documents across Reuters, The Jakarta Post, Setkab, Kompas, detik, CNA, KPK materials, and a few later retrospective pieces.
- Kept evidence that had a direct 2022 personal-nexus claim about Jokowi’s public truthfulness, asset disclosure, appointment choices, coalition management, or response to scrutiny.
- Rejected broad country-level corruption indicators, press-freedom summaries, and democracy datasets as standalone evidence for 7B because they do not by themselves show Jokowi’s personal integrity or dishonesty.
- Rejected out-of-period material from 2023-2024 about later dynastic allegations and election interference because the chapter request is for 2022.
- Rejected duplicate or near-duplicate reshuffle and third-term articles when they repeated the same factual core without adding a materially distinct claim.
- Considered KPK/LHKPN system pages and general anti-corruption pages, but kept only the Jokowi-specific asset-disclosure report because the generic pages were context only.
- Considered adding more press-freedom material, but much of it was better assigned to Chapter 4B or lacked a strong integrity nexus.

**Remaining gaps**
- Direct evidence of 2022 personal or family enrichment from office remains thin. I found a public asset disclosure, but not a strong independent audit of hidden benefit or conflicts.
- The reshuffle evidence is suggestive of patronage and coalition management, but it does not prove self-dealing or corruption on its own.
- There is solid evidence that Jokowi demanded truth-finding in major police/disaster cases, but less direct evidence on whether he corrected false claims in his own messaging beyond the election/term-limit statements already reused.
- More search would help if you want a stronger answer on hidden conflicts of interest, procurement favoritism, or any 2022 instance of Jokowi knowingly misleading the public for personal advantage.
