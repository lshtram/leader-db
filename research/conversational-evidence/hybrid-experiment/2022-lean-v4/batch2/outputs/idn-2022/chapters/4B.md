**Search and rejection summary**
- I considered roughly 30 documents across official transcripts, democracy/press-freedom reports, human-rights reports, legal summaries, Reuters-republished reporting, and local-language/independent coverage.
- I retained the sources that added a materially distinct claim about 2022 political-freedom behavior, especially term-limit restraint, media/digital pressure, and the December criminal-code turn.
- I rejected several duplicates or near-duplicates:
- Multiple term-extension articles were reduced to the clearest Reuters text plus the already-reused official Jokowi statements.
- Reuters Connect protest images were not retained because they were essentially captions and added less than the Reuters text and rights reports.
- Some Jakarta Post items were skipped when the same point was stated more cleanly in Reuters, HRW, or official legal summaries.
- I excluded 2023 and later retrospectives when they did not add target-year facts.
- I also avoided reusing every general democracy baseline source as if it were ruler-specific evidence; where a source mostly describes inherited conditions, I labeled it context.

**Remaining gaps**
- There is still no strong evidence that Jokowi personally manipulated the 2022 electoral machinery, courts, or election commission. The clearest evidence is that he rejected term-extension pressure rather than that he actively reformed the system.
- The largest negative evidence is legal and digital restriction: the ITE/MR5 environment, spyware suspicions, internet disruptions in Papua, and the new criminal code. Attribution is mostly to the state under Jokowi rather than to a personally directed order.
- I did not find decisive 2022 evidence of sustained institutional strengthening by Jokowi that would offset the restrictions, beyond his temporary intervention against term-extension talk and his earlier delay of the 2019 code draft.
- If you want a tighter attribution pass, the next useful search would be on the exact cabinet, ministry, and parliamentary chain behind the criminal-code and digital-control measures.
