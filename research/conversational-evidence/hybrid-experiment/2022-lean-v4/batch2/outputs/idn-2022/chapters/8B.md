Search and rejection summary
I reviewed roughly 25-35 documents across Setneg/Setkab, World Bank, IMF, Bank Indonesia, BPS, BKPM, Reuters, and The Jakarta Post. I kept the best non-duplicative evidence on program articulation, budget translation, appointments, investment/downstreaming, flagship projects, and 2022 macro outcomes.

I rejected or deprioritized:
- Archive/list pages that repeated the same story without adding new factual content.
- Duplicative syndications of Reuters or Setkab stories when a cleaner primary or higher-value version was already available.
- Generic country indicators and broad governance summaries that did not add Jokowi-specific program/execution evidence.
- Out-of-period 2023/2024 follow-ups when a 2022 or late-2022 source already established the relevant fact.
- Commentary pieces that were too interpretive when they lacked a concrete ruler-attributed action.
- Papers about political freedom, corruption, or rights that were relevant to other chapters but not to 8B’s execution question unless they directly tied to economic program implementation.

Remaining gaps
- I still have limited independent evidence on whether reshuffle choices and deputy-minister expansion improved execution rather than mainly managing coalition politics.
- I have partial, but not exhaustive, attribution on how much of the 2022 macro outturn came from Jokowi’s choices versus commodity windfalls, post-pandemic rebound, and BI policy.
- I have concrete examples of implementation on investment, IKN, and spending, but not a full audit trail from cabinet instruction to ministry-level delivery.
- I do not yet have strong external evaluation of whether 2022 downstreaming and investment-lubrication measures translated into durable productivity gains by year-end.

More search would help mainly if we wanted a tighter attribution of causal impact, especially on IKN, downstreaming, and investment-processing reform.
