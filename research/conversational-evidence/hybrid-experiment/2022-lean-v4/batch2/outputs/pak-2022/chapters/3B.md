**Search and rejection summary**
- I reviewed roughly 25-40 documents across HRW, Freedom House, Amnesty, CPJ, UN/OCHA/UN Women, Radio Pakistan, Euronews/Reuters, Dawn, and some blocked State Department excerpts.
- I kept sources that did one of three things: show Sharif-era repression, show concrete domestic-crisis protection, or establish authority/attribution.
- I rejected repeated syndications of the same Reuters stories when they did not add new facts.
- I rejected out-of-period material from 2021 and later 2023-2024 pages unless they clearly summarized 2022 conduct.
- I rejected broad economic or general governance pages that did not connect to physical safety, protest treatment, or vulnerability protection.
- The State Department custom excerpt pages were technically blocked/forbidden in this environment, so I did not rely on them.
- I did not use the client matrix.

**Remaining gaps**
- There is still no direct public record of Shehbaz Sharif personally ordering police or intelligence operations in the protest/media cases.
- I did not find a clean public record of a 2022 Sharif-led reform that improved oversight, complaint handling, or accountability for abuse.
- The strongest negative evidence is about protest and media suppression, not a national campaign of mass terror; the record looks like recurring repression and selective coercion rather than systematic state terror.
- The strongest positive evidence is flood relief and reconstruction planning; more search would help only if there are PMO, interior ministry, cabinet, or parliamentary records tying specific restraint or abuse-prevention decisions directly to Sharif.
