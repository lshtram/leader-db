**Search and rejection summary**
- I screened roughly 25-40 distinct documents across Reuters mirrors, Euronews, Indian Express, Dawn, MOFA, CTBTO-adjacent material, and a few broader Pakistan/region pages.
- I kept only items that either showed Sharif’s own peace messaging, showed foreign-policy conduct under his government, or documented concrete border incidents and ceasefire efforts.
- I rejected out-of-period items from before April 11, 2022 as direct Sharif evidence, including the March Afghanistan neighbor-fm meeting and the March joint statement, though they were useful as inherited-baseline context.
- I rejected repeated syndications of the same Reuters stories when they added no new factual content.
- I rejected broad profile pages and generic regional explainers that did not improve 2B attribution or conduct.
- I also kept the earlier March 2022 India missile incident only as reused crisis context from the prior chapter, not as new 2B evidence.

**Remaining gaps**
- There is still no clean PM-level public record of Sharif personally ordering a major cross-border military action or personally directing the Afghanistan channel.
- The April 2022 Afghanistan-airstrike allegations remain attribution-weak: the reporting suggests Pakistani action may have occurred, but the government denial means the personal chain of command is unresolved.
- The ceasefire with the local Pakistani Taliban was real, but the public record does not identify who represented Pakistan, so the ruler-specific peace credit is partial.
- More search would help only if it surfaces PMO, cabinet, NSC, or archival diplomatic records that tie a specific 2022 de-escalation or escalation decision directly to Sharif.
