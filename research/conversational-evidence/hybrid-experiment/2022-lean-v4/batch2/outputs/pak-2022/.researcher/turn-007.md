**Search and rejection summary**
- I screened roughly 30 documents across World Bank, WHO, UNICEF, WFP, FAO, IMF, the Government of Pakistan, and a few later retrospective pieces to separate 2022 action from later commentary.
- I kept 13 new source-claim units from 12 distinct URLs and reused 8 prior evidence items that genuinely fit this chapter.
- I rejected duplicate flood-response writeups from UNICEF, WFP, and World Bank when they repeated the same beneficiary counts or relief package without adding a new materially distinct claim.
- I rejected out-of-period follow-ups from 2023-2026 unless they directly described 2022 actions and added something materially new.
- I rejected baseline-only documents, such as generic Pakistan profile pages or macro indicators, when they did not show a ruler-linked welfare action.
- I treated local structured priors as context only: Pakistan’s 2022 baseline shows HDI 0.54, life expectancy 66.4, under-5 mortality 50.5, and BCG/DTP3 immunization at 95/85, but those figures were not used as independent evidence of Sharif’s action.
- A few government pages had cache/fetch issues during lookup; I did not rely on them when I could use accessible official or IGO sources instead.

**Remaining gaps**
- The biggest gap is ordinary-year, non-flood social policy under Sharif in 2022. Most strong evidence is emergency response after the floods, not steady-state welfare expansion.
- I still lack direct household-survey or administrative-outcome evidence showing how much 2022 decisions improved poverty, schooling, sanitation, nutrition, or health access by year-end.
- Attribution remains partly shared with provinces, the World Bank, UNICEF, WHO, WFP, and FAO. More work could sharpen what Sharif personally directed versus what his government inherited or coordinated.
- I do not yet have a clean, direct 2022 source on whether social protection and cash transfers were fully depoliticized and consistently reached the poorest groups across provinces.
- More search would help most on implementation audits, provincial delivery records, and 2022 budget execution for health, education, WASH, and social protection.
