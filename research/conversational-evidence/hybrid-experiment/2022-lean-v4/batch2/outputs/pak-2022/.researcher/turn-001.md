**Office and constraints**
- Shehbaz Sharif became Prime Minister of Pakistan in April 2022 after Imran Khan was removed in a no-confidence process.
- Constitutionally, the PM is the chief executive and is elected by the National Assembly, but in 2022 Sharif governed through a coalition with a slim parliamentary base.
- The main practical constraints in 2022 were coalition fragility, severe macro stress, the summer flood emergency, rights pressure around protest/repression, and the formal civil-military appointment process.

**Compact event map**
- April 9-11, 2022: Imran Khan is removed in a no-confidence vote; Shehbaz Sharif becomes PM and takes the oath of office.
- April 19, 2022: A 33-member coalition cabinet is sworn in.
- June 8, 2022: Energy shortages force a five-day workweek and broader conservation measures.
- July-August 2022: Inflation, reserve pressure, and currency stress intensify.
- August 6-19, 2022: Flood relief fund, cash relief, and emergency response become central government business.
- September-October 2022: UN and World Bank documents quantify the flood catastrophe and reconstruction burden.
- November 24-29, 2022: Sharif advises the top military appointments; Asim Munir takes command.
