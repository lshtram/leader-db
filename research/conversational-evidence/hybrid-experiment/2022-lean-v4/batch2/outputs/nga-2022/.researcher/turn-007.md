- Older 2018/2020 social-investment speeches and posts: useful background, but they predate the target year and were not needed once 2022 sources were available.
- Education pages and scholarship-board summaries that were either post-period, too general, or not as directly attributable as the sources retained.
- Currency-redesign implementation coverage that spills into 2023: out of period for this request.
- Duplicate or overlapping evidence from the same themes was pruned so the dossier would not repeat the same claim through multiple publishers.
