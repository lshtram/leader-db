**Remaining gaps**
- The record is strong on recurring abuse, protest repression, abductions, and weak accountability, but weaker on precise national command-chain attribution for every abusive act.
- I found evidence of remedy mechanisms and some compensation, but not enough to say those mechanisms were fully effective or independently robust.
- I did not find a clean target-year source showing Buhari personally created a major new oversight body in 2022; most positive evidence is partial, reactive, or institutional.
