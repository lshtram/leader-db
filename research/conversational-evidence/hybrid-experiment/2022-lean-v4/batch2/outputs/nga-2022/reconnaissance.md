**Office And Constraints**
Muhammadu Buhari was President and Commander-in-Chief in 2022, in the final year of his second term. The constitutional frame matters: executive power is vested in the president but is subject to the Constitution and laws, and Reuters reporting in June 2022 noted he was constitutionally barred from contesting again. In practice, his room to maneuver was narrowed by term limits, the APC succession process, legislative budget bargaining, courts, and the federal security/economic crises already in motion.
