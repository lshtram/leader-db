**Search and Rejection Summary**
- I considered roughly 40 documents across official Mexican sources, IMF, OECD, World Bank, CONEVAL, UNICEF, Reuters, and related secondary coverage.
- Kept the strongest non-duplicative items that trace the 2022 program-to-action-to-outcome chain: social transfers, scholarships, rural support, health institutional rollout, wage policy, inflation response, energy reform failure, and broader macro assessments.
- Rejected or downweighted duplicates where multiple mirrors or reposts repeated the same AMLO speech, budget claim, or press release without new material facts.
- Rejected generic context-only material that did not add ruler-specific effectiveness evidence, including broad corruption or freedom indicators, unless the source also tied them to a concrete AMLO program choice or implementation failure.
- Dropped out-of-period follow-ups unless they were clearly retrospective summaries of 2022 outcomes; those were used only when they added independent confirmation of 2022 effects.
- Some direct `gob.mx` pages were blocked on open and had to be handled through search snippets or mirrored official URLs; I kept the version that preserved the clearest locator and avoided duplicate citation of the same statement.

**Remaining Gaps**
- The biggest unresolved question is causal attribution: how much of the 2022 poverty and income improvement came from AMLO-backed policies versus U.S. labor demand, remittances, post-pandemic rebound, and other external conditions.
- There is still limited independent, audit-level evidence on whether flagship infrastructure projects stayed on time and on budget in 2022, especially Tren Maya, AIFA-related works, and aduanas modernization.
- I did not find strong independent evidence that austerity improved state efficiency rather than simply reallocating spending.
- Implementation quality for the social-program registry and targeting remains only partially evidenced; OECD and CONEVAL flag fragmentation and incomplete coverage, but I did not find a cleaner 2022 operational audit.
- If you want a tighter 8B judgment later, more search on project audits, federal procurement, and implementation reports would help.
