- I kept 13 distinct URLs and collapsed repeated coverage of the same Reuters stories where the syndication was materially identical.
- I excluded 2023 and 2024 material that was out of period for this chapter, including later judicial-reform writeups and broader retrospective democracy essays that did not add 2022-specific ruler conduct.
- I did not re-fetch Freedom House, V-Dem, RSF, or other structured datasets because the local prior already supplied the relevant country-year context.
- Some gob.mx pages were 403-blocked on direct open, so I relied on the visible search snippets and on other accessible presidential transcripts where the same point was documented more cleanly.
- I rejected generic commentary pieces that repeated the same press-freedom critique without adding a new 2022 ruler-attributed fact, and I did not pad the dossier with duplicate coverage of the same press or electoral-reform episode.

Remaining gaps:
- The biggest gap is 4B.4: I found strong evidence of pressure on independent institutions, but much thinner direct 2022 evidence that AMLO strengthened courts, the INE, INAI, or other oversight bodies when they constrained him.
- I have good evidence on media hostility and election-rule manipulation attempts, but less on opposition victories outside the electoral-reform fight and less on whether he systematically tolerated criticism in contexts other than the press.
- The surveillance record is persuasive, but part of it is retrospective to 2019-2021; more search could still find direct 2022 procurement, oversight, or prosecution records to sharpen attribution.
- More work could also look for 2022 budget, appointment, or legal-compliance records on INE, INAI, and CNDH to separate inherited weakness from ruler-period choices more cleanly.
