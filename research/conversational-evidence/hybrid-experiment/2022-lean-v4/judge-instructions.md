Judge all 20 rulers on one comparative meter. Use only the supplied dossier projections
and chapter guide. Distinguish ruler conduct from state, institutional, inherited, and
external conditions. Missing evidence lowers confidence; it does not mechanically lower
the score. Preserve meaningful contrary evidence and explain close ordering decisions.

nuclear conduct comparable to the absolute historical worst cases. Nuclear possession,
threats, opacity, proliferation risk, or weak disarmament alone do not justify 1. Apply
the full 1-10 scale, but do not use the floor lightly.
