**Search and rejection summary**
I considered roughly 30 documents across official Japanese government pages, MOFA releases, MOD strategy pages, Reuters-syndicated reporting, and regional/independent commentary.

Accepted and reused:
- Official PMO/Kantei pages on Ukraine, NATO, ASEAN/APEC, Japan-China, North Korea, and the December security documents.
- MOFA sanctions and U.N. related releases.
- Reuters-syndicated reporting on the defense buildup and reactions to the security strategy.

Rejected as duplicate or weak:
- Multiple archive/index pages that merely listed the same Kantei statements already captured in the live pages.
- Several 2023 and later retrospectives on Japan's defense shift and Ukraine diplomacy, because they were out of period for a 2022 chapter.
- Commentary/editorial pieces that restated the same facts without adding new attribution, contrary evidence, or a distinct claim.
- Broad security analyses that did not add Kishida-specific 2022 conduct beyond the official speeches and cabinet decisions already selected.
- Thin search results about Japan's alliance posture or generic pacifism that were redundant once the June, November, and December official records were retained.

**Remaining gaps**
- There is still no direct evidence of civilian harm, prisoner abuse, or unlawful use of force attributable to Kishida, because Japan was not conducting an external war in 2022.
- The record remains thin on internal cabinet deliberations and the extent to which Kishida personally balanced diplomacy against alliance reassurance and domestic hawks.
- I do not have a direct primary record showing implementation details for the December counterstrike/capability shift beyond the cabinet approval and immediate reporting.
- More search could help on Diet records, coalition debate, and regional consultations, but it is unlikely to change the core picture: Kishida's 2022 conduct was overwhelmingly diplomatic and anti-aggression in Ukraine, while his year-end security overhaul created the chapter's main mixed/contrary signal.
