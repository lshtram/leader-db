- One independent 2022 analysis that directly challenged Trọng's self-justification.
- Two retrospective obituaries/analyses for long-run personal-lifestyle and integrity reputation context.

Rejected or collapsed:
- Repeated VNA/VietnamNews/VietnamPlus mirrors of the same anti-corruption meetings when they added no materially distinct claim.
- Broad corruption-index/context pages without a Trọng-specific personal nexus.
- Repression, foreign-policy, and macroeconomic items that did not speak directly to personal honesty, self-dealing, or conflicts of interest.
- Post-2022 pieces that only repeated the same 2022 facts without adding a new claim.
- Promotional or commemorative items praising Trọng without traceable factual support for personal integrity.

- There is still no direct, independently verified evidence on Trọng's personal assets, gifts, family business interests, or declaration filings.

- The biggest unresolved question is attribution: the anti-corruption record is real, but it is still hard to separate personal integrity from factional discipline and regime maintenance.
