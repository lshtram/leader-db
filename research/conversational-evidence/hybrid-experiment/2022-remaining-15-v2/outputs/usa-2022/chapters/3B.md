**Search and rejection summary**
I screened roughly 30 documents across the White House / APP, DOJ, GovInfo, FBI/DOJ archives, and a small number of independent media reports. I kept sources that directly touched physical safety, intimidation, hate violence, policing accountability, gun violence, survivor protection, or vulnerable-group protection.

I rejected or deprioritized:
- Repeated versions of the same presidential speech when they added no new factual claim.
- Out-of-period items from 2021 or 2023+ unless they were used only as retrospective context.
- General domestic-policy material that did not connect to violence, abuse, intimidation, or protection.
- Purely symbolic remarks where there was no clear safety nexus.
- Broad political commentary that lacked a concrete domestic-safety claim.
- Overlapping election-threat and hate-crime pages when one stronger DOJ source already covered the same point.

**Remaining gaps**
- I do not have a direct 2022 source on federal prison or detention conditions that would materially change 3B attribution.
- I do not have a strong 2022 record showing whether Biden’s federal policing reforms were implemented effectively inside agencies, only that he issued them.
- Protest treatment is mostly evidenced by rhetoric and election-protection work, not by a large set of enforcement records.
- The biggest missing piece is a clearer before/after empirical link between Biden’s actions and year-end domestic safety outcomes.
- If you want a fuller 3B dossier, the next useful search would be on FBI domestic-terrorism cases, DOJ civil-rights enforcement outcomes, and any 2022 federal responses to abortion-clinic violence or anti-LGBTQ threats.
