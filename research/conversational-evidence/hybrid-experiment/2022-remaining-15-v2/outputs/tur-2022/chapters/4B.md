- I kept 11 distinct URLs and accepted 13 claim units because several sources covered multiple materially different 2022 facts.

- I rejected broad 2023 election-runoff explainers and the 2023 OSCE election final report as out-of-period for the core 2022 question set, even though they were useful for background.
- I also rejected nuclear and international-conflict material from the earlier chapters because it does not answer Chapter 4B.
