**Search and Rejection Summary**
- I reviewed roughly 25-35 documents across Reuters, IMF, World Bank, OECD, Turkish and international reporting, plus several dead-end searches on asset disclosure and family-wealth claims.
- I kept the sources that actually show a personal-nexus pattern: Erdogan's own repeated economic claims, loyalist appointments, media concealment around his inner circle, punishment of truth-tellers, and a documentary complaint involving Bilal Erdogan's access.
- I rejected broad corruption indices, media-freedom context, and authoritarianism reports when they lacked a direct ruler-specific honesty or self-dealing nexus.
- I rejected out-of-period items that were too far outside 2022 unless they were strictly retrospective about 2022 conduct.
- I did not treat generic repression from 3B/4B as integrity evidence unless it directly showed concealment, retaliation, or network benefit tied to Erdogan or his close circle.
- I found no high-grade, target-year forensic finding proving Erdogan's personal enrichment or hidden assets in 2022; that remains a major gap.

**Remaining Gaps**
- Direct financial evidence is thin: I did not find a 2022 asset declaration, beneficial-ownership record, or audit finding that firmly establishes personal enrichment by Erdogan himself.
- The strongest negative evidence is network-based rather than forensic: media control, loyalist appointments, and a family-linked access complaint.
- More direct evidence on campaign finance, procurement, gifts, or personal/family assets would materially improve 7B.3-7B.4.
- More evidence on Erdogan's corrections or lack of correction after false claims would help 7B.1-7B.2, but the existing 2022 record already shows repeated insistence on the low-rate line despite contrary evidence.
- If more search is worthwhile, it should target official financial disclosures, procurement records, and independent investigative reporting on the Erdogan family and presidency, not more generic corruption or repression context.
