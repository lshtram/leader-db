**Search and rejection summary**
- I considered roughly 30 documents across NATO, UN, HRW, Reuters/Euronews mirrors, Turkish official outlets, US State reporting, and a few secondary explainers.
- I rejected several Reuters mirrors because they repeated the same article without adding new facts.
- I rejected later 2023-2024 retrospective pieces when they did not improve the 2022 evidence on Erdoğan’s actual choices.
- I rejected several Turkish government items that only repeated the same Operation Claw-Sword justification, unless they added a materially distinct claim or locator.
- I rejected broad background pages on Syria, NATO, or the PKK when they were purely contextual and not ruler-attributable for 2022.
- I did not keep weaker or blocked items that could not be opened cleanly or only restated already-captured claims.

**Remaining gaps**
- The biggest gap is direct internal decision evidence: public records do not show Erdoğan’s private deliberations, legal review, or command-chain specifics for civilian-harm mitigation.
- Proxy-control evidence is still incomplete for the exact Turkish relationship to every SNA faction in 2022, even though the proxy-abuse pattern is well documented.
- The public record is thin on any Turkish compensation, investigation, or discipline after the 2022 Syria strikes.
- More searching could still help on parliamentary, court, or oversight records, but it is unlikely to overturn the basic 2022 picture: real diplomatic restraint in some venues, paired with a substantial and escalating cross-border Syria/Iraq military campaign.
