**Search and rejection summary**
I screened roughly 30 documents across IMF, OECD, World Bank, NATO, UN, Turkish Presidency/Strateji-Bütçe, TurkStat, the Trade Ministry, Reuters, and EBRD coverage. I kept the sources that showed a real chain from program to implementation to outcome, and dropped repeated Reuters rewrites of the same low-rate story, generic country-level macro indicators with no ruler-specific mechanism, and older 2021 precursor pieces unless they were needed to explain a 2022 action. I also treated broad post-2022 retrospectives as usable only when they explicitly summarized 2022 implementation or outcomes. A few Turkish official pages were only accessible as search snippets in this environment, so I used those snippets rather than forcing weaker duplicates.

**Remaining gaps**
The dossier is strongest on Erdoğan's macroeconomic program, investment planning, crisis-response measures, and a couple of foreign-policy wins. The main gap is direct, fine-grained evidence of 2022 implementation quality inside line ministries, procurement, or audit records, especially anything that would show whether specific projects were completed on time and within cost. More search would help if you want a tighter competence judgment on budget execution, project delivery, or whether personnel changes inside the economic team materially improved implementation.
