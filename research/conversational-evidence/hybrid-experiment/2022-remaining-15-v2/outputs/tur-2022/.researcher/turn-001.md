**Office, authority, constraints**
- Erdoğan was the **President of the Republic of Türkiye** in 2022.
- Under the 1982 Constitution as amended in 2017, the president is the **head of state** and holds **executive power**. The president can appoint ministers and senior officials, command the armed forces through the constitutional framework, and issue presidential decrees on executive matters.
- The constraints matter too: presidential decrees cannot regulate fundamental/political rights, cannot cover areas reserved exclusively to statute, and are subject to constitutional review.
- The presidency is a **5-year elected office**, with a **two-term cap**, and the National Assembly can trigger renewed elections by a **3/5 majority**. That makes 2022 a pre-election year inside a strongly presidentialized but still legally bounded system.
