Removed or de-prioritized items fell into these buckets:
- Duplicate coverage: multiple PCO and PNA pieces repeated the same SONA nuclear quote or the same 123-agreement launch.
- Out of period: 2023 123-agreement entry-into-force coverage, 2023 NuScale and Ultra Safe Nuclear pieces, and 2024-2026 cybersecurity/AI and nuclear-safety items were informative but not 2022 evidence.
- Weak / overbroad: generic climate, infrastructure, or science articles that did not add ruler-level existential-risk choice beyond what the SONA and treaty sources already established.
- Blocked pages: several PCO pages returned 403 or decoding errors when opened directly, so I relied on the search snippets for those rather than promoting lower-quality substitutes.
- Low-value repeats: later commentary on nuclear energy in general, without Marcos-specific choice or a 2022 timestamp, was not kept.
