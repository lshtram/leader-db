**Search and rejection summary**
I screened roughly 30 documents across the Philippines 2022 governing-program, budget, personnel, and macro-outcome trail. I kept the strongest non-duplicative items where they showed a direct Marcos nexus: explicit program statements, concrete appointments, budget allocations, administrative rollouts, and year-end outcome indicators.

I rejected or deprioritized:
- Duplicate coverage of the same personnel and budget events across multiple outlets, especially repeated reporting on Diokno, Balisacan, Bersamin, the 2023 budget, and the 8-point agenda.
- Generic country-level macro indicators that did not add a ruler-specific causal link beyond the PSA, DOF, or IMF summaries already selected.
- Out-of-period follow-ups from 2023-2026 that were not needed for the 2022 dossier.
- Sources that were too close to official self-promotion without adding a new materially distinct claim.
- A few direct PCO or PNA fetches that were blocked or returned internal/403 errors; where possible I used mirrored or more accessible versions of the same official material.
- Articles that repeated the same fact pattern without adding a new implementation step, appointment, or outcome signal.

**Remaining gaps**
- I still do not have audit-level or internal-government evidence that Marcos personally monitored implementation quality across all major 2022 priorities.
- I do not have a clean, ruler-attributable 2022 line of evidence that separates his effect from the post-pandemic rebound, commodity-cycle conditions, and inherited fiscal/monetary management.
- The strongest outcome evidence is aggregate and still partly lagged; it shows growth, inflation pressure, debt management, and labor-market recovery, but not the full causal chain from policy to durable result.
- More searching would help most on implementation follow-through in agriculture, infrastructure, and education, especially if you want subnational delivery evidence or formal audits rather than press/budget records.

Overall, the 2022 record is strongest on explicit program-setting, appointment of a capable economic team, and early conversion into budgets and administrative actions. The weaker part is independent proof of durable end-of-year outcome gains that can be cleanly attributed to Marcos rather than to inherited recovery dynamics.
