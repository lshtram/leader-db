**Office, authority, constraints**
- Marcos was sworn in on **June 30, 2022** as the **17th President of the Philippines**. The 1987 Constitution makes the president the repository of executive power, gives control over executive departments, and makes the president commander-in-chief, while also constraining the office through a fixed six-year term, congressional lawmaking/budget power, and judicial review.
- In 2022, the practical shape of the office was also defined by a large electoral mandate, a cooperative vice president in Sara Duterte, an incoming technocratic cabinet, and a political environment still marked by the Duterte legacy, inflation, pandemic recovery, rights disputes, and South China Sea tension.
- The most important inference here is that Marcos had broad formal executive authority, but he inherited a constrained environment rather than a blank slate.
