**Office, Authority, Constraints**
- AMLO was president of Mexico in 2022, having taken office on **1 December 2018**. Source: the official presidential inauguration record.
- His formal executive authority was broad: promulgate and execute laws, appoint and remove cabinet officials, direct foreign policy, and exercise command over the armed forces.
- In practice, he was constrained by Congress, the Supreme Court, the National Electoral Institute (INE), federal-state fragmentation, organized-crime violence, inflation/pandemic aftershocks, and a large gap between formal control and policy passage.
