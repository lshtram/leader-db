**Search and Rejection Summary**
- I considered roughly 30 to 40 documents across AMLO's official 2022 speeches and reports, SHCP/SEP/Salud implementation notices, IMF/OECD/World Bank surveillance, and Reuters/Infobae/Forbes/Bloomberg Línea reporting.
- I kept sources that linked AMLO's program to concrete budgets, institutional rollout, completed projects, or independently described outcomes.
- I rejected repeated social-program pages that restated the same beneficiary counts without adding a new implementation mechanism or outcome.
- I rejected broad Mexico macro or poverty explainers when they did not add ruler-period attribution beyond what the IMF/OECD/World Bank sources already provided.
- I rejected later 2023 or 2024 follow-ups when the 2022 source already captured the relevant implementation or failure.
- I rejected articles that only restated that Mexico had low growth, high corruption, or institutional weakness without connecting those conditions to AMLO's own program choices or execution.
- I also rejected some infrastructure commentary that was too speculative about eventual completion to add more than the AIFA and Dos Bocas pieces already included.

**Remaining Gaps**
- The main unresolved question is durability: 2022 shows large-scale mobilization, but the evidence is thinner on whether the flagship infrastructure and social programs produced lasting productivity gains by year-end.
- More independent evaluation of LEEN, IMSS-Bienestar, and PACIC would help separate real operational delivery from official self-reporting.
- The clearest weaknesses are still investment climate, energy-policy uncertainty, and cost overruns on flagship projects; stronger evidence on how much of that was AMLO's direct control versus inherited constraints would improve attribution.
- I still do not have a clean, ruler-specific causal chain from the 2022 anti-inflation package to household welfare net of fiscal cost and macro side effects.
- If you want this chapter judged later, the next highest-value search would be independent audits or evaluations of AIFA, Dos Bocas, IMSS-Bienestar, and the education program results after the 2022 rollout.
