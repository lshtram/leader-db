Buhari was Nigeria’s President and Commander-in-Chief throughout 2022, operating inside a federal system with real constitutional limits: the National Assembly, courts, federal-party rules, and, on military matters, Senate constraints and statutory oversight. The 2022 local prior already gives the structural baseline I would not re-fetch: persistent conflict/insecurity, rights repression, weak rule of law, corruption risk, and poor governance capacity. I used the web only for ruler-specific attribution, official statements, and narrative context.

**Retained sources**
1. [Past Heads of State and Presidents](https://statehouse.gov.ng/past-heads-of-state-and-presidents/) - The State House, undated roster page. Fact: lists Muhammadu Buhari as President from 29 May 2015 to 29 May 2023, so 2022 is squarely within his presidency. Period fit: exact office anchor. Ruler attribution: primary official roster. Limitations: no policy detail or event coverage. Contrary points: none on tenure.
2. [Nigeria 1999 (rev. 2023) Constitution](https://www.constituteproject.org/constitution/Nigeria_2023) - Constitute Project, 2023 revision of the constitution text. Fact: executive power is vested in the President; the President is Head of State and Commander-in-Chief; external combat deployment and war powers are constrained by the Senate/National Assembly; presidential term is four years and term limits apply. Period fit: stable legal framework governing 2022. Ruler attribution: legal authority/constraint source, not a performance source. Limitations: mirror text, not an official gazette scan. Contrary points: the constitution defines power but does not prove how fully Buhari exercised it.

Fact: Buhari speaks as sitting President, calls it his last Independence Day address, and foregrounds ASUU, education funding, health, floods, and climate stress. Period fit: direct 2022 self-report. Fact: HRW says 2022 was marked by high insecurity, the Owo church killings, Kuje prison break, the Abuja-Kaduna train attack, abuses by security forces, and restrictions on expression/media. Period fit: very strong year-level synthesis. Ruler attribution: independent critical overview. Limitations: rights-focused, broad, and not a ruler-specific causal model. Contrary points: it highlights harms and abuses more than countervailing government gains.
