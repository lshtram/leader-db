**Search and rejection summary**
I reviewed roughly 30 documents across Setkab, Kemenkeu, BPS, Bank Indonesia, World Bank, IMF, WHO/UNICEF, BPK, Reuters/AP/CNA/Jakarta Post, and several duplicated translations of the same government releases. I kept only sources that added a materially distinct claim about Jokowi’s 2022 governing program, implementation, coordination, adaptation, or measurable outcomes.

Rejected or not separately accepted:
- Duplicate English/Indonesian versions of the same Setkab or WHO release when they added no new fact.
- Generic macro dashboards and country pages that repeated the same 2022 indicators without new ruler-specific linkage.
- Multiple stories on the June 2022 cabinet reshuffle that repeated the same facts; I kept one official explanation and one independent critical report as the best pair.
- Broad political-freedom or corruption materials that did not help 8B because they lacked program-to-implementation linkage.
- Post-2022 follow-ups that were only background unless they provided a clean retrospective on FY2022 outcomes.
- Source clusters around the same themes, such as subsidy reallocation, poverty, and inflation, when one authoritative official release plus one independent cross-check was enough.

**Remaining gaps**
- The strongest evidence is about program definition, budget/fiscal management, social assistance rollout, IKN initiation, and macro stabilization. Direct evidence on Jokowi personally supervising day-to-day implementation is thinner than evidence on the administration as a whole.
- Several favorable outcomes were aided by external tailwinds, especially commodity prices and post-pandemic reopening. That means outcome gains are not pure ruler skill signals.
- The record is weaker on whether underperforming implementers were systematically replaced across the whole program portfolio, beyond the June 2022 reshuffle and a few visible crisis responses.
- I did not find a clean, independent audit of Regsosek’s 2022 implementation quality by year-end; more search would help if you want deeper attribution on targeting and delivery.
- Education recovery remained incomplete, so additional source work on learning-loss remediation and subnational delivery would improve confidence in the human-capital part of the dossier.
