**Remaining Gaps**
- `5B.2` is still the weakest lens. I have evidence that Jokowi reshuffled the cabinet and that the reshuffle had political-crisis and coalition elements, but I do not have a clean 2022 source showing he systematically appointed clearly superior economic professionals over loyalists.
- `5B.4` and `5B.5` would benefit from more direct firm-level or competition-policy evidence, especially on monopolies, connected business privileges, procurement discipline, and market entry.
- `5B.8` remains only partly covered by headline poverty, inequality, and labor-market data. More direct household-income or regional-distribution evidence would help.
- Attribution is mixed on the upside: growth, tax receipts, and stability were helped materially by reopening, commodity prices, and inherited buffers, so the positive macro evidence does not translate into full ruler credit by itself.
- The corruption picture is still largely indirect: CPI and cabinet-crisis reporting point to weak anti-corruption performance, but a stronger 2022 ruler-specific anti-capture audit would help separate inherited corruption from Jokowi-era choices.
