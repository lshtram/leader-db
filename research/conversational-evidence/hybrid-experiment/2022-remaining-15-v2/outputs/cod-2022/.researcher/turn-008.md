**Remaining gaps**
- Direct household-level outcome evidence is still thin. I have strong program and response evidence, but less on measured changes in mortality, learning, or nutrition attributable to Tshisekedi in 2022.
- Budget execution and implementation quality in the social sectors remain under-documented relative to announcements and donor commitments.
- There is still limited independent evidence on subnational distribution, especially east versus other regions, beyond crisis-zone reporting.
- Durable institutional change is visible in data-planning and social-safety-net design, but I do not yet have strong post-implementation audits proving permanence.
- If you want a tighter dossier, the next useful search would be audits, household surveys, and sector evaluations for health, education, and social protection rather than more press releases.
