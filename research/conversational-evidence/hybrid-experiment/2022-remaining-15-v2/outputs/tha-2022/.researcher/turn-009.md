I kept sources that directly tied to Prayut's 2022 program, budget execution, coalition control, appointments, or year-end political vehicle shift, and I rejected items that were only generic macro context, duplicated the same claim through another mirror, fell outside 2022 without a clear retrospective payoff, or lacked a direct Prayut nexus. I also skipped sources that mostly repeated country-level indicators rather than documenting a ruler-specific program or implementation step.

**Remaining gaps**
1. Direct evidence on Prayut personally setting the whole program is still incomplete. The record is strongest on public statements, budget passage, coalition control, and late-2022 re-election maneuvering.
2. Some implementation evidence is still sectoral rather than comprehensive. EV, tourism, fuel-price relief, and investment promotion are well evidenced, but there is less direct coverage of broader administrative execution outside those lanes.
3. Outcome attribution remains mixed. Investment promotion improved in some sectors, but overall applications were projected to fall later in 2022, so more evidence would help separate real competence from favorable sub-sectoral momentum and external reopening effects.
