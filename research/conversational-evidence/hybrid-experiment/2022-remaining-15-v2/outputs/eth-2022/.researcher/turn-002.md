- Out-of-period material from 2024-2026 on Ethiopia's later nuclear-energy ambitions, later cybersecurity strategy pages, and later PMO/social media pages.
- Broad opinion/editorial pieces and generic Africa/nuclear explainers that did not add Ethiopia-specific 2022 value.
- Blocked or low-value mirrors that repeated the same legal or news text without better attribution.
- General nonproliferation fact sheets that were useful for background but not more useful than the official treaty records already kept.
