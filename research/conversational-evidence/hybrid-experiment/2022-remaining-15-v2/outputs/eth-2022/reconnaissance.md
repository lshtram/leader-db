**Office, authority, constraints**
- Abiy Ahmed’s formal office in 2022 was Prime Minister of Ethiopia, not president. Under the Ethiopian constitution, the PM is the chief executive, chairs the Council of Ministers, and is commander-in-chief of the armed forces.
- His authority is real but not unilateral: the PM and cabinet are responsible to the House of Peoples’ Representatives, ministers are approved by the House, and emergency powers require parliamentary approval.
- In 2022 his room to maneuver was constrained by the ongoing northern war, federal/regional fragmentation, humanitarian pressure, and external mediation by the AU/IGAD/UN.

Period fit: the decisive late-2022 turning point. Ruler attribution: shows Abiy’s government negotiating under external mediation and with unresolved constraints. Limitations: summary of a shared text, not implementation proof. Contrary points: the agreement itself leaves key issues open.

Period fit: strong inherited-conditions and post-agreement source. Ruler attribution: directly ties the state to the transitional-justice follow-through.
