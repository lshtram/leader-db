**Search and rejection summary**
I screened roughly 30 documents across these buckets:
- UN Secretary-General statements and press releases on Ethiopia
- AU Commission and AU mediation statements
- Reuters reports mirrored by Euronews and Al Jazeera
- HRW and Amnesty conflict/atrocity reporting
- WFP, UNHCR, UNICEF, UNAIDS, and UNDSS operational updates
- ENA English, Amharic, and Tigrinya material
- A few later retrospectives and summaries for implementation context

I rejected:
- Duplicate Reuters mirrors that repeated the same fact without adding a materially distinct claim.
- Out-of-period retrospectives from 2023-2026 when they only restated the same 2022 event.
- Broad commentary or explainer pieces that did not add Ethiopia-specific 2022 detail.
- Generic humanitarian background pages that were less useful than the direct UN operational updates kept above.
- Non-2B material on nuclear risk, corruption, or domestic liberties that belonged to other chapters.

**Remaining gaps**
- There is still no direct public source showing Abiy Ahmed personally making a 2022 external-war choice against a neighboring state. The local prior's zero internationalized-conflict figures matter here, and the dossier remains a limited-exposure 2B case rather than a classic interstate-war case.
- I did not find a public source proving direct Abiy command over Eritrean troop movements or a formal government acknowledgment of every allied atrocity in 2022.
- The weakest area is exact internal decision-making: public evidence shows the peace track and civilian harm, but not his private deliberations or command chain details.
- If you want to tighten the attribution and implementation side further, the next useful search would be archived Ethiopian-government or ENA Amharic/Tigrinya items from November-December 2022 on the Pretoria implementation steps, especially any statements on disarmament, federal force redeployment, and service restoration.
