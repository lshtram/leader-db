**Search and rejection summary**
- I screened roughly 30 documents across IMF, World Bank, UNICEF, WHO, WFP, Bangladesh government/parliament budget pages, local media, and retrospective poverty/education sources.
- I rejected repeated coverage of the same budget speech when a cleaner or more specific excerpt already existed, and I did not duplicate the same claim across multiple republished articles.
- I rejected generic country pages and dashboards when they added no Bangladesh-2022 claim beyond the more specific UNICEF or World Bank pages.
- I treated 2025-style dashboard pages and 2024-2026 updates as context only unless they explicitly reported 2022 results from household surveys or country-year indicators.
- I discarded blocked or inaccessible full texts when their accessible summaries already carried the needed claim: the WFP annual-country-report PDF download and the IMF eLibrary full text both returned access errors, so I relied on accessible snippets and corroborating pages instead.
- I treated Reuters inflation coverage and similar macro-news items as redundant once the IMF and World Bank sources had already captured the inflation, reserve, and policy-tightening story more precisely.
- I treated the UNICEF and WFP 2022-2026 strategy pages as useful mainly for baseline, inherited conditions, and durability, not as proof of single-year Hasina personal causation.
