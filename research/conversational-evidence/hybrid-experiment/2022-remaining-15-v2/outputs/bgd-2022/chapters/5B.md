Remaining gaps
The main gap is fine-grained ruler attribution: the dossier shows the Hasina government’s economic posture, but not always Sheikh Hasina personally choosing each rate move, reserve decision, or appointment. 5B.2 is still the thinnest lens because the record shows a technocratic appointment, but not a clear pattern of empowered independent economic professionals versus loyalists. Household-level distribution data for 2022 is also still thinner than headline GDP and poverty data, so more search would help on median wages, rural-urban gaps, and budget execution versus announcement.
