Search and rejection summary
I screened roughly 30 documents across the State Department, HRW, Guardian, CPJ, RSF, Internet Society, Prothom Alo, The Daily Star, bdnews24, BSS, and a few Reuters/Al Jazeera derivatives. I kept the strongest non-duplicative claims and dropped repeated coverage of the same December 2022 crackdown, 2023-2024 retrospectives that were outside the target year, and structured Freedom House/RSF/Net pages whose underlying indicator content was already supplied in the local prior. I also treated a few government/party pages as context only when they were just self-justifying rhetoric rather than independently useful evidence.

Remaining gaps
The main gap is direct personal attribution. The record strongly supports a Hasina-government pattern of restriction, but there is little clean 2022 evidence that Sheikh Hasina herself ordered a specific arrest, content block, or police action. Institutional capture also remains better evidenced than formal court or legislature strengthening, so 4B.4 is thin. If you want a tighter dossier, the best next targets are election commission records, court rulings, and parliamentary proceedings from 2022, plus any contemporaneous official directives on protest permissions or digital-content blocking.
