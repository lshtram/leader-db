**Remaining gaps**
The dossier is strongest on flood-driven crisis response, maternal/child welfare, and the design of social-protection and health programs. The main gaps are:
- Direct evidence that Sharif personally drove routine, non-crisis improvements in health, education, nutrition, or housing beyond public launches and emergency relief.
- Independent evaluations of whether the 2022 relief and recovery measures actually improved outcomes for households by year-end.
- More precise attribution between federal direction, provincial execution, and donor financing.
- Better 2022 evidence on school re-entry, learning recovery, and longer-run nutrition impacts after the floods.

More search would help, especially on budget execution, independent program evaluations, and district-level outcome data.
