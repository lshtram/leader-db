**Search and rejection summary**
I reviewed roughly 30 documents across official Pakistani pages, UN/CTBTO/IAEA pages, SIPRI, NTI, Reuters wires and syndications, Dawn, the Indian Express, AP, Al Jazeera, Tribune, The Guardian, and other regional coverage.

I rejected or deprioritized items that were:
- duplicates of the same Reuters wire on the March 2022 missile incident,
- generic opinion pieces or explainers that added no new factual claim,
- out-of-period or mostly retrospective material when a 2022 source was available,
- weak fact-checks or social-media explainers that did not improve attribution,
- broad economic or flood stories that were useful for other chapters but not for 1B,
- inaccessible cache-miss pages or repeated pages that added no new evidence.

Examples of collapsed/removed clusters:
- Multiple March 2022 missile-incident articles from Reuters-syndicated outlets were reduced to one independent incident source plus one Pakistani official response source.
- Older FAS Pakistan notebook items were considered but not retained because they were pre-2022 background and less useful than SIPRI/NTI for this chapter.
- Several generic peace-or-diplomacy articles about India or the US were not retained because they did not add nuclear-specific authority, restraint, or safety detail.

**Remaining gaps**
- No direct 2022 evidence surfaced of a Shehbaz-chaired NCA meeting focused on nuclear doctrine, custody, or safety reform.
- No independent source showed a substantive change to Pakistan's launch posture, command-and-control rules, or fissile-material policy under Sharif in 2022.
- There is still no material evidence that Sharif personally handled dual-use cyber, biological, or AI risk policy in 2022.
- Evidence for 1B.6 and part of 1B.7 remains thin: I found no strong 2022 example of Sharif resisting proliferation pressures or appointing expert nuclear reformers in a way that can be cleanly attributed to him.

Net takeaway, without scoring: Sharif had formal nuclear-chairmanship authority and publicly projected restraint, but the 2022 record I found is mostly inherited-posture management and crisis de-escalation signaling, not proof of structural risk reduction.
