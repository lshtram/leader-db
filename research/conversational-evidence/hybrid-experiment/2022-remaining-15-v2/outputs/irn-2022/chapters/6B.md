**Remaining gaps**
- Direct Khamenei-attributed 2022 welfare decisions remain thin. Most evidence is regime-level or ministry/UN partnership level, not a personal welfare agenda order from the Supreme Leader.
- Distribution and dignity evidence is better for refugees, girls, women, and children than for the overall population. More Iran-specific household survey work would help on who benefited versus who was left behind.
- There is still limited hard evidence on whether 2022 social-policy improvements were durable or merely compensatory amid sanctions, inflation, and COVID aftereffects.
- If you want a tighter dossier on 6B.4 and 6B.9, the next useful step would be Persian-language budget/execution records or ministry audits showing whether the 2022 program expansions were actually financed and sustained.
