- I rejected broader 2023-2026 retrospectives when they were not directly tied to the 2022 period, except where they summarized 2022 shutdowns or accountability gaps.
- I did not re-fetch Freedom House, RSF, V-Dem, WGI, BTI, or Polity because the local prior already supplied the structured baseline and the instructions prohibited re-fetching those datasets.
- I did not chase out-of-period election stories because 2022 had no national contest that changed the core picture.
- I avoided padding with unrelated macro or nuclear material because it does not answer Chapter 4B.
