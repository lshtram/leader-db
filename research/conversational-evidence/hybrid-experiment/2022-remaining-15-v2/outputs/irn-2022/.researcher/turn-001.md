**Office / Authority / Constraints**
- In 2022, Ali Khamenei was Iran’s Supreme Leader, which is the office above the presidency in the constitutional order.
- The constitution gives the Leader command of the armed forces, war/peace powers, appointment and dismissal authority over top judicial, military, and media posts, decree power for referenda, and the power to confirm or dismiss the president.
- Constraints are real but internal to the system: the Leader must satisfy qualification criteria, the Assembly of Experts can remove him if he loses them or becomes incapable, and Article 113 still leaves the president as the highest official for ordinary executive business outside the Leadership’s domain.

**Major 2022 Events / Inherited Conditions**
- The defining 2022 event was the Mahsa Amini crisis, which began after her death in custody on 16 September 2022 and rapidly became nationwide unrest.
- Khamenei publicly backed security forces, framed the unrest as enemy-driven, and endorsed a harder line.
- Rights groups documented lethal force, arrests, and internet restrictions; the UN later created an independent fact-finding mission.
- On the nuclear file, the IAEA kept pressing Iran over undeclared uranium traces, and Iran escalated enrichment to 60% at Fordow in November.
- On inherited conditions, the economy entered 2022 under sanctions pressure, inflation, and a constrained recovery; the World Bank still described gradual recovery, but with persistent bottlenecks and inflationary pressure.
