# 2022 comparative judge calibration

Apply the chapter guide to the full 20-ruler cohort. Do not treat the cohort's worst
ruler as an automatic score of 1; the 1–10 scale is historical and absolute.

For Chapter 1B, reserve score 1 for the guide's realized-catastrophe floor: an actual
nuclear detonation against people or a military target, or a genuinely equivalent
realized nuclear catastrophe. Threats, escalation, arms buildup, aggression, or
authoritarianism do not by themselves meet that floor.

Use missing or weak ruler-attributed evidence to reduce confidence and trigger review,
not to infer either a low or high score.

Return the complete strict schema, but keep every prose field concise. Use no more than
three decisive positive items, two decisive negative items, and two contrary-evidence
items per ruler. Keep each explanation to one short sentence and each rationale or
anchor field to at most three short sentences. Confidence must use the required 0–100
scale. Every numeric score must cite at least one decisive evidence item.
