**Compact Event Map**

- December 2021 to January 2022: Scholz enters 2022 as chancellor of a three-party coalition, with pandemic aftereffects, flood recovery, and a large governing agenda already on his desk.
- February 2022: Russia invades Ukraine; Scholz halts Nord Stream 2, then announces the Zeitenwende, sanctions, a 100 billion euro Bundeswehr fund, and a broader defense reset.
- March to June 2022: coalition and parliamentary bargaining shape the special fund and weapons policy; the government begins treating energy security as a central crisis.
- June 2022: gas emergency level is activated; Scholz folds the defense reset into a broader alliance strategy tied to the EU, G7, and NATO.
- July to September 2022: the government keeps reducing Russian energy dependence while inflation stays high and domestic relief measures expand.
- Late 2022: the energy price brake and related relief packages become central instruments for social and economic stabilization.
