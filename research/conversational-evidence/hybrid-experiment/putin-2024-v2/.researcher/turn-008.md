**Search and rejection summary**
- I reviewed roughly 30 documents across `rg.ru`, `tass.com`, `theprint.in`, `investing.com` Reuters syndications, `yle.fi`, `genproc.gov.ru`, plus background items from OCCRP, IStories, Interfax, Reuters Connect, AP, Yle, and several duplicate Reuters mirrors.
- I kept the strongest 2024, Putin-specific, personal-nexus items and rejected the rest as follows.
- Duplicate or redundant removals: multiple syndications of the same Reuters story on the same decree, appointment, or disclosure issue; I kept one clean copy per event.
- Out-of-period removals: 2017-2022 asset-investigation pieces were treated as background only and not retained as target-period evidence, unless they were directly reused as context.
- Weak or context-only removals: broad corruption indices, generic repression reports, and country-level freedom/press material were not retained unless they connected to a Putin-specific act, decree, appointment, or disclosure decision.
- Blocked/inaccessible removals: a few Interfax items and some republication pages were not reliably fetchable; I used other copies or the official text instead.
- Irrelevant removals: Ukraine battlefield reporting, war-economy macro stories, and general election-result stories were excluded unless they spoke directly to concealment, conflicts of interest, or appointment/favoritism.
- I also dropped broad “Putin is rich” background claims that lacked a 2024 anchor or direct documentary trail; the Yle and official disclosure/decree items were stronger and more period-fit.

**Remaining gaps**
- Direct proof of Putin’s own beneficial ownership remains indirect. The best evidence still runs through proxies, relatives, and secrecy rules rather than a public, independently audited asset register.
- I found strong evidence of concealment and patronage, but little affirmative evidence of candor, correction, or openness to adversarial scrutiny in 2024.
- The public 2024 disclosure is a formal counterpoint, but because later decrees made declarations non-public, it does not resolve the transparency question.
- More search would help if you want tighter proof on corporate registries, leaked ownership chains, or Russian-language investigative work linking specific assets and companies to Putin personally.
