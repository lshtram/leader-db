**Search and Rejection Summary**
- I reviewed roughly 30 documents across official Kremlin transcripts and decrees, Reuters wire reporting syndicated through Investing, ThePrint, MarketScreener, and Yahoo, plus IMF and Bank of Russia material.
- I retained the strongest 2024-specific, Putin-attributable, program-to-implementation/outcome items and rejected the rest as follows.
- Duplicate syndications: multiple copies of the same Reuters story on Belousov, the defense purge, the budget, and battlefield updates were collapsed to the cleanest accessible version.
- Out-of-period removals: older background pages, especially the 2014 World Bank Russia Economic Report page I opened by mistake, were excluded because they did not fit the 2024 target period.
- Weak or generic removals: broad Russia macro pages, generic country landing pages, and non-Putin-specific commentary were dropped unless they linked directly to his 2024 program, appointments, budgets, or outcomes.
- Blocked or inaccessible removals: a few Kremlin budget pages timed out; I relied on the official snippet where it was sufficient and otherwise excluded the page.
- Relevance filtering: battlefield stories were kept only when they showed operational progress or expansion relevant to the ruler’s revealed war program, not merely because they reported conflict.
- I also discarded items that were too ceremonial or too remote from the 2024 execution chain, such as one-off educational visits that did not add much beyond the stronger education and health implementation records already retained.

**Remaining Gaps**
- The biggest gap is causal attribution: there is good evidence that Putin set the program, moved resources, reshuffled personnel, and achieved visible wartime and macro outputs, but less evidence isolating his own competence from force size, inherited capacity, or the distortions of a war economy.
- Independent verification of some social and health outcomes is still thin compared with the official Russian reports.
- I have stronger evidence on budgets, appointments, and military progress than on whether the 2024 national-project apparatus improved long-run productivity, demographics, or service quality.
- More search would help most if you want tighter evidence on procurement performance, defense-industrial throughput, or independent audits of 2024 social-program execution.
