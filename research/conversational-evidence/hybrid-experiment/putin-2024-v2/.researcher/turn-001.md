**Office / Constraints**

- `Putin` was the sitting president of Russia in 2024 and began a new six-year term on `May 7, 2024` after the March election.
- Formally, the Russian presidency is the head of state role, with decree power, foreign-policy direction, commander-in-chief authority, and emergency/martial-law powers. The formal constitutional constraints are the six-year term, constitutional supremacy, and the impeachment framework.
- In practice, 2024 constraints were much more political than institutional: war, sanctions, repression, security shocks, and labor/economic strain narrowed the real room for maneuver.
