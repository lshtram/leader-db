**Bottom Line**
Xi Jinping in 2022 held the core office bundle of CCP General Secretary, PRC President, and CMC Chairman. The strongest 2022 evidence shows a high degree of personal concentration of authority through the 20th Party Congress, but also real constraints: elite/state implementation bottlenecks, zero-COVID backlash, economic slowdown, and sustained external human-rights criticism. That last point is an inference from the source set, not a single source claim.
