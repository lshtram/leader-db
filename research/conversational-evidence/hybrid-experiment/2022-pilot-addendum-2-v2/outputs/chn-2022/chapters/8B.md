**Search and Rejection Summary**
I screened roughly 30-plus documents across `gov.cn`, `english.scio.gov.cn`, `stats.gov.cn`, the NHC, CBIRC, Reuters/MarketScreener, and a few rights-monitoring sources.

Retained because they were directly useful for 2022 program, implementation, adaptation, appointments, or outcomes:
- 20th Congress report and objectives pages.
- State Council stabilization package and implementation/inspection follow-up.
- CBIRC credit allocation and housing-completion guidance.
- NHC COVID optimization and the December management downgrade.
- NBS annual communiqué, jobs release, and rural-income release.
- Xi anti-corruption speech and personnel-control briefings.
- Reuters on leadership reshuffle and ally-dominated top bodies.

Rejected because they were duplicates, too generic, out of period, or weakly attributable:
- Repeated congress directory pages that only linked headlines.
- 2023-2024 follow-ups that would have drifted outside the 2022 ruler-period.
- Generic macro commentary with no clear operational link to Xi's program.
- Purely rhetorical congress summaries that repeated the same claims without adding implementation detail.
- Duplicate republications of the same Reuters item.

**Remaining Gaps**
- Independent, non-official evaluation of whether the 33-measure stabilization package actually improved implementation quality across provinces.
- Better evidence on whether loyalist-heavy appointments improved competence or mainly tightened political control.
- More direct audit of the COVID policy reversal's welfare costs and whether the year-end shift was timely enough to count as effective adaptation rather than late correction.
- More subnational evidence on housing delivery, youth employment, and service quality.

More search would help, especially on local audits, provincial implementation reports, and independent empirical studies of 2022 policy execution.
