**Office, Authority, Constraints**
Olaf Scholz was the ninth Federal Chancellor, elected on 8 December 2021, and in 2022 he led the first federal traffic-light coalition of SPD, Greens, and FDP. His office had real steering power under Article 65 of the Basic Law, but he was still constrained by Bundestag majority math, coalition bargaining, ministerial autonomy, and the fact that removal requires a constructive vote of no confidence.

**Compact Event Map**
- 8 Dec 2021: Scholz takes office with a three-party coalition and a reform-heavy coalition agreement.
- 24-27 Feb 2022: Russia invades Ukraine; Scholz answers with the Zeitenwende speech, sanctions, arms support, and the 100-billion-euro Bundeswehr fund.
- Spring 2022: Parliament turns the initial announcement into law and the Ukraine policy becomes more concrete.
- Summer 2022: the energy shock, inflation pressure, and coalition tradeoffs dominate domestic politics.
- 29 Sep 2022: the government unveils the 200-billion-euro economic shield and gas-price brake.
- Nov 2022: criticism sharpens over slow defense procurement and whether the Zeitenwende is moving fast enough.
