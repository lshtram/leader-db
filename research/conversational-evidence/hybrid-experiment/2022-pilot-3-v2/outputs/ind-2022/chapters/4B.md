Search and rejection summary
I reviewed roughly 30 documents and kept 13 distinct URLs. I rejected or collapsed:
- Duplicate coverage of the same incidents across Reuters, ReutersConnect, NDTV, India Today, Times of India, Hindustan Times, and The Economic Times when one stronger source already captured the material fact.
- Purely visual or caption-only ReutersConnect items because they added little beyond the text reports.
- Out-of-period pages from 2023-2026 that described later events rather than 2022 conditions.
- General country-profile material that repeated the local structured priors without adding ruler-specific attribution.
- Broad opinion pieces and advocacy pages when a stronger official, court, or investigative source was available for the same point.
- Evidence about national security, international diplomacy, or domestic crime that did not bear directly on political freedom.
- Several election-adjacent items from the Election Commission and PIB that were useful background but not as strong as the direct Modi congratulatory messages for contestability and transfer.
- Later press-freedom summaries that duplicated HRW, RSF, Amnesty, or Access Now on the same 2022 media and digital-control pattern.
