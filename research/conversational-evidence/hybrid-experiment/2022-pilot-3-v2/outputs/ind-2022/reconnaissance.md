**Office**
Narendra Modi was India’s Prime Minister in 2022, so the relevant office is the head of government, not head of state. Constitutionally, the PM heads the Council of Ministers, the President acts on ministerial advice, and the Council is collectively responsible to the Lok Sabha. In practice, Modi entered 2022 with a BJP-led parliamentary majority from 2019, so he had substantial agenda-setting power, but still faced real constraints from Parliament, the Supreme Court, state-level policing and administration, and electoral backlash.
