**Gap status**
- PM GatiShakti implementation vs. slogan: partly resolved.
- Independent logistics-cost or project-completion proof: partly resolved, not fully recoverable from the sources I found.
- Attribution between Modi, RBI, states, and private actors: partly resolved, still mixed.
- Labor-market quality and household opportunity: not resolved in a positive sense; the best independent data still show weak conditions.
- Durable end-of-period improvement: partly resolved, but only as a broader trajectory, not as a clean ruler-only causal finding.

**What remains weak**
- I did not find a credible independent 2022 evaluation that directly attributes logistics-cost reduction to Modi’s 2022 GatiShakti rollout.
- The IMF and World Bank material improves the outcome picture, but the causal chain still runs through shared institutions and longer-run reforms.
- The labor-market evidence remains a constraint on any claim of broad-based execution success.

**Blockers**
- The IMF eLibrary staff report endpoint hit a 403, so I used the IMF news feature and World Bank data instead.
- The World Bank Human Capital page is a benchmark table, not an evaluation study, so it is useful for outcome context but not for causation.
- I did not add duplicate 6B/7B sources from the prior index because they were already in the dossier and did not materially change the 8B gaps.
