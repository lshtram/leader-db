**Search and Rejection Summary**
- I considered roughly 30 documents across official Kremlin transcripts, Reuters/Euronews stories, World Bank and IMF material, Defence Department analysis, and a few later retrospective pieces.
- Kept the sources that most directly traced Putin's 2022 program from declaration to action and outcome: invasion goals, tactical retreats/reframing, mobilisation, personnel changes, industrial tasking, and battlefield reverses.
- Rejected as duplicates or low-yield:
- Multiple later 2023-2025 recaps on Gerasimov, Surovikin, and war performance that repeated the same battlefield narrative without adding 2022-specific execution evidence.
- Broad Russia country indicators and governance metrics already present in local priors, because they are context rather than ruler-specific effectiveness evidence.
- General corruption, repression, and casualty articles that did not illuminate program-to-action-to-outcome causality for 8B.
- Blocked or impractical Kremlin page opens that timed out; where the direct source was still useful, I relied on the search snippet or search-result summary rather than inventing a citation.
- Out-of-period follow-ups were excluded unless they were only being used to describe a 2022 event already within the target period.

**Remaining Gaps**
- Direct 2022 internal performance data from the Russian military, procurement system, and industrial ministries remain thin. The dossier shows mobilisation and adaptation, but not full delivery metrics.
- Attribution is strong for Putin's broad program, weaker for some specific command and procurement moves that were executed by Shoigu, Gerasimov, Surovikin, or ministries on his behalf.
- I did not find a clean, independent 2022 audit of whether the late-2022 defence-industrial and UAV directives actually translated into sustained battlefield advantage before year-end.
- If you want tighter separation between "frantic adaptation" and "genuinely effective execution," the best next sources would be Russian-language budget/execution records, defence-industrial procurement data, or serious academic histories of the 2022 campaign.
