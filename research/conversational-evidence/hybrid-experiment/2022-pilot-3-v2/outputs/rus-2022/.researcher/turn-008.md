**Search and Rejection Summary**
- I considered roughly 30 documents across Reuters, HRW, Treasury, FactCheck.org, Guardian/OCCRP, and Euronews-syndicated Reuters pieces.
- Kept only items with a direct personal-nexus to honesty, self-dealing, concealment, obstruction, punishment of investigators, or a clearly attributable pro-professional counterexample.
- Rejected or downgraded:
  - Generic Russia corruption indices and repression indicators already present in the local priors, because they are country-level context rather than proof of Putin’s personal integrity.
  - 2021 Pandora Papers material and 2024/2026 follow-ups, because they were out of the 2022 target year or too far from the target period.
  - Broader war / human-rights reports that did not show a direct ruler-specific honesty or self-dealing nexus.
  - Some Reuters items on invasion predictions and mobilization were considered but not elevated because the FactCheck synthesis and the more direct prosecution / wealth sources were cleaner for 7B.
- I reused the positive Nabiullina appointment evidence instead of duplicating it with a new Reuters item, because it was already in the dossier and genuinely fits 7B.5.
