**Office / Constraints**
Putin was the President of the Russian Federation in 2022. Formally, that office made him head of state and supreme commander-in-chief, gave him decree power, appointment powers, and martial-law authority, while the Constitution also imposed formal constraints such as Federation Council involvement for martial law and a high impeachment threshold. That is the legal frame; it does not by itself prove how weak or strong those checks were in practice.
