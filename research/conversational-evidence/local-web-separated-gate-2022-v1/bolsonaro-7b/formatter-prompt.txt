## 1. Orientation

### Authority and practical control

Jair Bolsonaro was president throughout 2022, the final year of his 2019–2022 term. He formally controlled the federal executive, presidential communications and premises, ministerial appointments, senior appointments in the Federal Police, Federal Highway Police and intelligence apparatus, execution of the federal budget, and the constitutional pardon power. He did not control Congress, the courts, the TSE, the TCU, state governments, independent prosecutors, or all police forces.

In practice, coalition dependence—especially on the Centrão—constrained appointments and spending. Military officers, political allies and personally trusted aides nevertheless occupied unusually prominent federal posts. These circumstances support attribution where Bolsonaro personally spoke, issued a decree, used presidential resources, selected or protected an official, or was adjudicated to have led relevant conduct; they do not make every ministerial or coalition scandal his own.

### Inherited baseline

Brazil entered 2022 with:

- A functioning but contested anti-corruption and accountability framework: CGU, TCU, Federal Police, prosecutors, courts, the Access to Information Law, election auditing, and active independent media.
- Long-standing clientelism and corruption risks predating Bolsonaro.
- The Car Wash task force terminated in 2021 amid both legitimate due-process criticism and concern that anti-corruption enforcement was weakening.
- Existing allegations involving Bolsonaro relatives and associates, but no established 2022 finding that Bolsonaro personally obtained proceeds from the older “rachadinha” or property matters. BTI expressly said that, as of its review period, Bolsonaro himself had not been implicated in a corruption scandal, while relatives and associates were suspected. [BTI 2022, pp. 11 and 34](https://bti-project.org/fileadmin/api/content/en/downloads/reports/country_report_2022_BRA.pdf)

### Shocks and constraints

The election, post-pandemic recovery, inflation, food insecurity and Russia’s invasion of Ukraine shaped the year. Congress and the courts constrained executive action. These shocks may explain policy reversals or fiscal tradeoffs; they do not explain unsupported electoral claims, conversion of official events into campaign assets, concealment of family-access records, or a later-adjudicated attempt to remain in power.

### Information environment

Criticism and investigation remained possible. The TSE, STF, TCU, CGU, prosecutors, police, journalists and fact-checkers produced extensive records. This creates greater visibility than in a closed system, so article volume alone should not be treated as severity. The strongest evidence here consists of ruler statements, contemporaneous orders, judicial findings and the later final criminal judgment—not complaint counts.

A methodological limitation: the workspace was read-only and exposed no filesystem-reading command, so the local guide and registry could not be inspected directly. The supplied local-first findings were used as the starting corpus, and each accepted web source was opened or its underlying official search extract inspected.
