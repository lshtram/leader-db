## 2B.1 Research Note: Vladimir Putin, Russia, 2023

**Working judgment:** **Mostly no.** In 2023 Putin used diplomatic language and met with third parties, but on the core Ukraine question the available evidence shows conditional, maximalist terms rather than compromise or de-escalation. I found no solid 2023 evidence that he accepted a credible peaceful alternative before relying on force.

### Search gap

I did **not** find reliable 2023 evidence that Putin accepted a balanced ceasefire, territorial compromise, or other clearly credible peaceful alternative before continuing the war. The strongest diplomatic statements were conditional, framed around Ukraine accepting Russian demands, or directed at third-party mediation rather than real compromise.

### Bottom line

**Mostly no.** Putin did use diplomatic channels and language in 2023, but the record does not show him preferring compromise when peaceful alternatives existed. The evidence points to negotiations as a tool under coercive conditions, with force remaining the default.
