## Bottom Line

The best 2023 evidence is mixed but asymmetric: Putin remained in control and still had domestic approval, macroeconomic resilience, and regional diplomatic activity, while 2023 also brought major negative signals from the ICC, the Wagner mutiny, and a sustained crackdown on dissent and civil society.
