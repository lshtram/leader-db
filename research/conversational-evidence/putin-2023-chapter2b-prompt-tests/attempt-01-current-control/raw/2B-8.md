## 2B.8 Research Note: Vladimir Putin, Russia, 2023

**Working judgment:** **Mostly no.** The macro evidence shows military spending and mobilization being used to sustain the war, intimidate neighbors, and project strength, with some real-security justification language but little sign that this was the dominant function.

### Search gap

I did **not** find reliable 2023 evidence that Putin’s military spending or mobilization was primarily structured around transparent, bounded defensive requirements. The strongest public justification was “army needs,” but the surrounding record points to war-fighting, elite loyalty, and intimidation of neighbors.

### Bottom line

**Mostly no.** The 2023 pattern is military spending and mobilization as wartime sustainment, coercion, and strength projection, with defensive rationale present but not dominant.
