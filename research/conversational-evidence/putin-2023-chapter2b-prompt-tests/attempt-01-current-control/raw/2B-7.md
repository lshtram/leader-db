## 2B.7 Research Note: Vladimir Putin, Russia, 2023

**Working judgment:** **Mostly no.** I found rhetorical openness to talks and some third-party peace mediation, but no credible 2023 evidence that Putin pursued a real ceasefire, lawful settlement, confidence-building package, or post-conflict reconciliation when feasible. The pattern is conditional negotiation language without meaningful concessions.

### Search gap

I did **not** find reliable 2023 evidence that Putin accepted a verifiable ceasefire, implemented meaningful confidence-building measures, accepted lawful post-conflict settlement terms, or pursued reconciliation. The available evidence is mostly conditional rhetoric: talks, yes; compromise, no.

### Bottom line

**Mostly no.** Putin’s 2023 peace language was real but tactical. It did not translate into a credible ceasefire or settlement pathway when such alternatives were available.
