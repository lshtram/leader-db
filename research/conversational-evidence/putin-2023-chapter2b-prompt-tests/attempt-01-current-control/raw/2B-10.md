## 2B.10 Research Note: Vladimir Putin, Russia, 2023

**Working judgment:** **No.** Putin left regional/international relations **less peaceful, less stable, and less lawful** in 2023. There were some formal diplomatic signals and multilateral contacts, but the year’s concrete moves were more important: continued war-related coercion, the suspension of New START, tactical nuclear deployment in Belarus, and an ICC arrest warrant.

### Search gap

I did **not** find reliable 2023 evidence that Putin left the regional/international order more peaceful, stable, and lawful than he inherited it. The available positive evidence is mostly formal diplomacy and rhetoric; the negative evidence is concrete and cumulative.

### Bottom line

**No.** Putin preserved some diplomatic access with China and CIS partners, but 2023 overall made regional and international relations **more dangerous and less lawful**, chiefly through continued war escalation, arms-control rupture, and coercive nuclear signaling.
