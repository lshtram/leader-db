## 2B.2 Research Note: Vladimir Putin, Russia, 2023

**Working judgment:** **Yes, overwhelmingly.** The 2023 record shows continued war expansion/coercion and only conditional diplomacy. I found no credible evidence that Putin accepted a genuine peaceful alternative when one was available.

### Search gap

I did **not** find reliable 2023 evidence that Putin accepted a credible peace deal, withdrew from occupied territory, or dialed back coercion when peaceful alternatives existed. The strongest “pro-diplomacy” items were conditional statements, not evidence of real compromise.

### Bottom line

**Yes.** In 2023 Putin continued and justified the Ukraine war with cross-border coercion and escalatory nuclear signaling. Diplomatic language existed, but it was subordinate to force and maximalist terms.
