## 2B.5 Research Note: Vladimir Putin, Russia, 2023

**Working judgment:** **Mostly no.** The best 2023 evidence shows crisis containment in the Wagner mutiny, but not sustained restraint of proxies or acceptance of responsibility for proxy atrocities. The clearest counterevidence is Kremlin patronage of Wagner and continued proxy abuse in Africa.

### Search gap

I did **not** find reliable 2023 evidence that Putin clearly restrained Wagner, other proxies, or arms recipients from atrocities, or that he accepted responsibility for their foreseeable conduct. The record instead shows a mix of plausible deniability, continued patronage, and only narrow crisis containment during the mutiny itself.

### Bottom line

**Mostly no.** Putin contained one internal proxy crisis to avoid immediate bloodshed, but the broader 2023 record does not show genuine restraint, accountability, or responsibility for proxy abuses and destabilization.
