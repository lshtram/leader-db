I kept this to a small set of accepted claims from primary or highly credible sources. I did not map repo-specific question IDs in this isolated workflow, so I list the rubric lens instead.

**1. 2023 baseline: war-time repression inside Russia**
- **Title:** Russia: War's Supersized Repression
- **Publisher:** Human Rights Watch
- **Date:** January 12, 2023
- **URL:** https://www.hrw.org/news/2023/01/12/russia-wars-supersized-repression
- **Claim summary:** HRW said the full-scale invasion of Ukraine triggered a new drive to eliminate public dissent in Russia, including war-censorship laws that punished calling the conflict a war, criticizing the invasion or Russian armed forces, or reporting on war crimes and Ukrainian civilian casualties.
- **Question IDs / lens:** domestic repression, information control, regime coercion, wartime governance
- **Type:** direct 2023 context

**2. March 2023: international legal isolation**
- **Title:** ICC judges issue arrest warrant for Putin over war crimes in Ukraine
- **Publisher:** Reuters, republished by Investing.com
- **Date:** March 17, 2023
- **URL:** https://www.investing.com/news/world-news/icc-judges-issue-arrest-warrant-against-putin-over-alleged-war-crimes-3033607
- **Claim summary:** The ICC issued an arrest warrant for Putin over the alleged unlawful deportation of Ukrainian children, sharply worsening his international standing and travel freedom.
- **Question IDs / lens:** international standing, legality/legitimacy, wartime conduct
- **Type:** direct 2023 event

**3. June 2023: serious elite challenge**
- **Title:** Russia's Putin says he let mutiny continue to avoid bloodshed
- **Publisher:** Reuters, republished by Investing.com
- **Date:** June 26, 2023
- **URL:** https://www.investing.com/news/world-news/russias-putin-says-he-let-mutiny-continue-to-avoid-bloodshed-3113594
- **Claim summary:** After Wagner’s armed mutiny, Putin said he had let it run to avoid bloodshed and claimed it reinforced national unity. The event itself showed a rare internal challenge to his authority.
- **Question IDs / lens:** elite cohesion, coercive control, regime stability, crisis management
- **Type:** direct 2023 event

**4. October 2023: war economy framing**
- **Title:** Putin says Russia's economy can cope with higher defence spending on war
- **Publisher:** Reuters, republished by Investing.com
- **Date:** October 5, 2023
- **URL:** https://www.investing.com/news/economic-indicators/putin-says-russias-economy-can-cope-with-higher-defence-spending-on-war-3191542
- **Claim summary:** Putin argued that Russia could absorb higher military spending despite sanctions. Reuters noted defense spending would be about one-third of 2024 budget expenditure, while the rouble had already fallen sharply in 2023 and labor shortages persisted.
- **Question IDs / lens:** economic management, sanctions resilience, war mobilization, material capacity
- **Type:** direct 2023 event with 2023 context

**5. 2023 economic performance was still positive**
- **Title:** Росстат представляет первую оценку ВВП за 2023 год
- **Publisher:** Rosstat
- **Date:** February 7, 2024
- **URL:** https://rosstat.gov.ru/storage/mediabank/18_07-02-2024.html
- **Claim summary:** Rosstat’s first estimate put Russia’s 2023 GDP growth at 3.6 percent year over year, with manufacturing and capital formation contributing strongly.
- **Question IDs / lens:** economic performance, state capacity, war-economy durability
- **Type:** retrospective measure of 2023

**6. 2023 war conduct produced heavy civilian harm**
- **Title:** Head of UN Human Rights Monitoring Mission in Ukraine presents the latest human rights report
- **Publisher:** United Nations in Ukraine / OHCHR
- **Date:** October 6, 2023
- **URL:** https://ukraine.un.org/en/248423-head-un-human-rights-monitoring-mission-ukraine-presents-latest-human-rights-report
- **Claim summary:** The UN reported that from February 1 to July 31, 2023, hostilities killed at least 1,028 civilians and injured 3,593, with 86 percent of casualties in government-controlled territory likely caused by Russian attacks; it also documented ongoing arbitrary detention and torture by Russian authorities.
- **Question IDs / lens:** wartime conduct, civilian protection, coercion, external legitimacy
- **Type:** direct 2023 period evidence

**Bottom line**
- 2023 looked like a year of entrenched wartime rule: external isolation, internal coercion, a major but contained mutiny challenge, and a war economy that still produced positive headline growth.
- The strongest favorable evidence is economic resilience; the strongest critical evidence is repression, civilian harm, and the ICC warrant.
