## Bottom line

2023 looks like a year of **durable personal control under severe external pressure**: international legal isolation widened, repression deepened, and the Wagner episode exposed vulnerability, but Putin still retained the machinery of state, projected continuity, and benefited from a short-term wartime economic rebound.
