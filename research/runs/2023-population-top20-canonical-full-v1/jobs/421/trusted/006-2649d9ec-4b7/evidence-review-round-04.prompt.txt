Attribution is governmental, not solely Scholz. [Federal Government arms FAQ](https://www.bundesregierung.de/breg-de/service/fragen-und-anworten/faq-waffenlieferungen-2027766), sections “How are weapons deliveries decided?” and “What does the end-use clause mean?”

This is an official claim, not independent verification. [Bundestag Israel-export report](https://www.bundestag.de/presse/hib/kurzmeldungen-1009274), opening paragraphs.

This is contrary procedural evidence, not proof of German illegality. [European Parliament question P-003630/2023](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/doceo/document/P-9-2023-003630_EN.html).
