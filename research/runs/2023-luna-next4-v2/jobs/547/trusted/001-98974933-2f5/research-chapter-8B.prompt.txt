2. Trace conversion into priorities, resources, laws, institutions, appointments,
   timelines, enforcement, and measurable milestones.
3. Examine whether the state, party, military, coalition, bureaucracy, territorial
   network, or informal machinery actually mobilized, coordinated, and followed
   through; include bottlenecks and implementation gaps.
4. Assess whether personnel were capable for the assigned program. Ideological or
   coercive operators may be effective implementers in this narrow chapter even
   when their conduct is reprehensible elsewhere.
5. Compare promised/revealed objectives with implementation and outcomes after
   realistic lags, inherited trends, peer conditions, shocks, and resources.
6. Trace learning, replacement, reallocation, crisis response, resistance
   management, short-term gains, reversals, durability, and end-of-period position.

# Nguyễn Phú Trọng, Vietnam, 2023

## 1. Overview

Nguyễn Phú Trọng was General Secretary of the Communist Party of Vietnam (CPV), serving his third term. In Vietnam’s party-state system, this was the country’s most influential office: the Party controls senior appointments, strategic policy, the legislature, security institutions, and the state apparatus. The president, prime minister, National Assembly chair, and other Politburo members remained important institutional actors; implementation depended on the government, ministries, provincial authorities, police, military, and courts.

Trọng’s influence was therefore extensive but collective rather than fully personal. He set the political line, chaired the anti-corruption campaign, shaped senior personnel decisions, and represented Vietnam in major strategic diplomacy. His major 2023 priorities were party discipline, anti-corruption, political stability, economic recovery, national security, and “bamboo diplomacy.” The government maintained peace and macroeconomic stability, expanded ties with the United States, and continued rapid development, but growth fell below target and political freedoms remained severely restricted.

Information quality was uneven. Official Vietnamese sources provide detailed evidence of formal decisions and government objectives but omit or sanitize dissent, coercion, corruption networks, and policy failures. Independent reporting and international human-rights organizations provide important counterevidence, but access to victims, defendants, journalists, officials, and local records is restricted.

## 2. Evidence records

### Record 1 — Trọng’s formal office and practical authority

**Fact.** Nguyễn Phú Trọng was General Secretary of the CPV Central Committee throughout 2023 and the dominant figure in Vietnam’s party-state system. The General Secretary was not the head of government in the constitutional sense, but the Party controlled the state’s strategic direction and senior personnel. His authority was mediated by the Politburo, Central Committee, prime minister, president, National Assembly, military, police, and provincial party structures.

**Why it matters.** Attribution of national outcomes to Trọng should focus on decisions and campaigns directed through the Party, while avoiding treating every administrative outcome as his personal act.

1. **Orient.** Establish the ruler's formal and practical authority, the inherited situation, major external shocks or constraints, and the information environment.
2. **Plan coverage.** For every selected lens, identify the important favorable, adverse, disputed, and exculpatory possibilities that research should test.
3. **Find the main record.** Begin with strong overviews and syntheses. Identify the consequential decisions, laws, budgets, appointments, statements, implementation, corrections, and outcomes.
4. **Verify material claims.** Open the underlying sources. Use precise laws, official records, audits, judgments, datasets, scholarship, monitoring, and credible reporting where they establish or challenge an important claim. Assess each source’s authority, independence, method, proximity, and date.
5. **Test the account.** Look for contrary evidence, independent corroboration, relevant local-language material, source dependencies, and alternative explanations. Distinguish inherited conditions and country context from conduct attributable to the ruler.
6. **Deepen where useful.** Repeat steps 3–5 while new searches add a material fact, a stronger source, a missing perspective, or a necessary correction. Finish when further searching mainly repeats the established record or reaches a concrete access barrier.
7. **Prepare the handoff.** Organize the evidence by underlying fact and map each developed claim to every selected lens it supports.

## Evidence records

Create one record for one source supporting one material claim. If one source supports several materially different claims used downstream, create a separate record and locator for each claim. Give records about the same underlying fact or event the same `underlying_fact_key`, and identify reports that depend on the same investigation, dataset, wire story, or official claim.

Each developed record includes:

- one precise factual claim and why it matters;
- source title, publisher, date, direct URL, and stable locator;
- target-period, inherited, or later-retrospective status;
- the ruler-attribution basis;
- source limitations, dependencies, and credible contrary evidence;
- independent corroboration; and
- every selected question ID the claim supports.

Keep accepted evidence, corroboration, reused sources, uninspected leads, rejected sources, and blocked sources in clearly labelled groups. Explain the source landscape when a lens remains unanswered.

4. the labelled source groups;
5. source counts and remaining research gaps; and

## Discovery/extraction boundary

A separate discovery stage has already built the candidate source catalogue shown in Resources already identified. This turn is evidence extraction and gap-directed inspection, not the primary broad-discovery pass. Open and inspect at least 12 of the most promising candidates across independent source families when access permits, then extract located facts. Perform additional searches only for a concrete factual, attribution, contrary-evidence, or source-family gap exposed during inspection. Evidence-unit targets are depth diagnostics and must not be used as proof that the source universe is saturated. For every candidate actually inspected, emit one SOURCE_CANDIDATE_JSON line using the discovery contract and change access_status to accepted, rejected, opened, or access_blocked. accepted means at least one SOURCE_CLAIM_JSON evidence record was retained from that same URL; rejected means it was inspected but provided no usable evidence. If access prevents the inspection target, emit INSPECTION_SATURATION_BLOCKER_JSON with chapter_id, a nonempty documents_attempted array, and a concrete limitation.
