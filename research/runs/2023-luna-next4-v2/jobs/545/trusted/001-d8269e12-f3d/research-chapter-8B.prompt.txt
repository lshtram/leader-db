3. Examine whether the state, party, military, coalition, bureaucracy, territorial
   network, or informal machinery actually mobilized, coordinated, and followed

4. Assess whether personnel were capable for the assigned program. Ideological or
   coercive operators may be effective implementers in this narrow chapter even
   when their conduct is reprehensible elsewhere.
5. Compare promised/revealed objectives with implementation and outcomes after
   realistic lags, inherited trends, peer conditions, shocks, and resources.
6. Trace learning, replacement, reallocation, crisis response, resistance
   management, short-term gains, reversals, durability, and end-of-period position.

# Recep Tayyip Erdoğan — Türkiye, 2023

## 1. Overview

Recep Tayyip Erdoğan was Türkiye’s president throughout 2023 and leader of the Justice and Development Party (AK Parti). He won the 28 May presidential runoff, while the AK Parti-led People’s Alliance retained a parliamentary majority after the 14 May elections. The presidential system concentrated executive authority in the presidency; Erdoğan therefore had strong influence over government policy, senior appointments, security institutions, foreign policy, and public administration.

Important limits remained: parliamentary approval was required for legislation and some international commitments; courts and constitutional institutions retained formal powers; coalition partners, especially the Nationalist Movement Party (MHP), mattered politically; markets, NATO, Russia, the EU, and earthquake-related international assistance constrained choices; and municipal governments retained local responsibilities. In practice, the European Commission assessed executive checks and balances as ineffective, with accountability operating primarily through elections.

The central events of 2023 were the February earthquakes, the presidential and parliamentary elections, continued military operations in Syria and Iraq, the collapse of the Black Sea Grain Initiative, Sweden’s NATO accession process, and a late-year shift toward tighter monetary policy after years of unorthodox economic management.

The principal underlying source opened and extracted below is the European Commission’s *Türkiye Report 2023*, which covers June 2022–June 2023 and includes government, international-organization, NGO, and comparative-index evidence. It is especially useful for institutions, rights, elections, public administration, corruption, migration, security, and foreign policy. [European Commission, *Türkiye Report 2023*](https://ab.gov.tr/siteimages/birimler/kpb/trkiye_report_2023.pdf)

## Workplan

1. **Orient.** Establish the ruler's formal and practical authority, the inherited situation, major external shocks or constraints, and the information environment.
2. **Plan coverage.** For every selected lens, identify the important favorable, adverse, disputed, and exculpatory possibilities that research should test.
3. **Find the main record.** Begin with strong overviews and syntheses. Identify the consequential decisions, laws, budgets, appointments, statements, implementation, corrections, and outcomes.
4. **Verify material claims.** Open the underlying sources. Use precise laws, official records, audits, judgments, datasets, scholarship, monitoring, and credible reporting where they establish or challenge an important claim. Assess each source’s authority, independence, method, proximity, and date.
5. **Test the account.** Look for contrary evidence, independent corroboration, relevant local-language material, source dependencies, and alternative explanations. Distinguish inherited conditions and country context from conduct attributable to the ruler.
6. **Deepen where useful.** Repeat steps 3–5 while new searches add a material fact, a stronger source, a missing perspective, or a necessary correction. Finish when further searching mainly repeats the established record or reaches a concrete access barrier.
7. **Prepare the handoff.** Organize the evidence by underlying fact and map each developed claim to every selected lens it supports.
