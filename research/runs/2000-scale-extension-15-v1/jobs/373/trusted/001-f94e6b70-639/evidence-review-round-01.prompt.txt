The case is not fully closed, but the available search pass was constrained by:

- limited availability of archived 2000 pages;
- many search results pointing to later Chávez/Maduro periods;
- retrospective sources that merge Chávez’s full tenure rather than isolate 2000;
- official and opposition narratives likely requiring corroboration;
- insufficient exact-page extraction in this execution profile;
- inability to verify several promising observer and government documents.

This means silence is not probative. The principal unresolved issue is not a lack of country-level indicators but a lack of directly attributed, target-period narrative evidence.

The local evidence establishes a country-year baseline of:
