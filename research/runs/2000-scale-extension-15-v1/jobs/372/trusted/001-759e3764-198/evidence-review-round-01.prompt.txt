## 7. Strongest contrary evidence and adversarial checks

- **Counter-evidence to a "positive effectiveness" reading:** Operation Return to Life (E-008) is the strongest single contrary fact. Even granting IMF-program success, the Ecevit cabinet authorized a violent operation that resulted in ≥30 prisoner deaths. For Chapter 3B, this is decisive and will dominate weaker positive factors.
- **Counter-evidence to a "negative freedom" reading:** Helsinki candidacy (E-018/E-050/E-051), coalition stability (E-014), Çalışlar regret (E-045), and Sezer acceptance (E-007) all push the chapter toward a mixed-to-positive reading. A purely negative Chapter 4B reading would over-weight the F-type raids and ignore these.
- **Counter-evidence to "personal corruption" reading:** No specific 2000 personal-corruption finding against Ecevit was located in available sources. The country-institutional corruption scores (LF047–LF050) are not attributable to him personally. The 7B chapter must avoid inflating country-corruption priors into personal-integrity claims.
- **Survivorship and visibility:** The F-type prison crisis is one of the most-covered human-rights events of 2000 globally; it is unlikely to be a sampling artifact. The Marmara earthquake recovery is under-covered in the available searches because the tool was rate-limited; this is a known under-coverage rather than a finding of absence.
- **Closed-environment caveat:** Ecevit's coalition included MHP (nationalist) and ANAP (Yılmaz-led, with documented prior corruption). Internal coalition discipline and cabinet papers from 2000 are not directly accessible in this research pass. Future passes should consult Turkish Grand National Assembly committee minutes, Court of Accounts reports, and the EU Commission's first Regular Report on Turkey (November 2001) for finer-grained execution detail.
