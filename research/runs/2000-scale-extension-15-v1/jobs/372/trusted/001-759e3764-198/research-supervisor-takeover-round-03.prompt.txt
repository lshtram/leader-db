## 2. Ruler-period reconnaissance (broad)

Ecevit's 1999–2002 tenure was defined by:
- 57th Government coalition (DSP/MHP/ANAP) — first stable coalition in years, formed after the April 1999 elections that capitalized on the Feb 1999 capture of PKK leader Abdullah Öcalan.
- Severe 1999 Marmara earthquake (Aug 1999) — recovery dominated social policy and fiscal pressure through 2000.
- Dec 1999 IMF Stand-By Arrangement (3-year, ~$15 bn including supplemental Nov 2000 add-on) — pegged disinflation program.
- Dec 1999 Helsinki European Council: Turkey granted EU candidate status.
- May 2000: election of Ahmet Necdet Sezer as President, ending Demirel's tenure after Ecevit-led constitutional-amendment push to allow Demirel re-election failed (April 2000 legislative votes).
- Aug 2000: Constitutional court/presidential crisis over military-influenced decree on religious headscarf regulation — Sezer twice rejected decrees forwarded by Ecevit.
- 20 Oct 2000: arrest of HADEP executives (legal pro-Kurdish party).
- 19 Dec 2000: "Operation Return to Life" — simultaneous military raids on 20 prisons to break hunger strikes against F-type prisons; 30 prisoners + 2 gendarmes killed; many immolated.
- 22 Nov 2000: First 2000–2001 financial crisis (liquidity crunch in overnight markets).
- Dec 2000: IMF supplemental ~$7.5 bn facility.
- Ecevit's health visibly deteriorating through 2000 (recurrent neurological issues); coalition coordination weakening.
