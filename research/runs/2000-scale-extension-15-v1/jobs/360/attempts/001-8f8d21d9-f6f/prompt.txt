- renewed economic growth;
- lower corruption;

- lower crime;
- fiscal order and spending restraint;
- labor-law reform;

The inherited context included recession, unemployment, substantial debt/deficit pressures, and fragile coalition cohesion.

The principal ruler-specific event discovered for 2000 was the Senate vote-buying scandal surrounding labor reform. Vice President Carlos Álvarez resigned on 6 October 2000 after publicly denouncing corruption and criticizing de la Rúa’s handling of the investigation. The allegation later became the subject of a corruption trial; separate later reporting indicates de la Rúa was ultimately cleared/acquitted, which is material contrary evidence. The exact court decision was not obtained during this pass.

Economic policy in 2000 centered on spending restraint, tax increases, maintaining peso-dollar parity, labor flexibility, and IMF-supported stabilization. The severe December 2001 protest repression and resignation crisis are outside the target year and were not treated as target-year evidence.
