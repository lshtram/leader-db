# 8. Continuation priorities

The highest-value continuation would retrieve at least two genuinely new units for every under-covered chapter:

1. **1B:** Independent FAS/SIPRI evaluation of 2023 US arsenal/posture and arms-control outcome.
2. **2B:** UN/ICRC/CRS material on Ukraine and Gaza arms transfers, civilian harm, conditions, and peace alternatives.
3. **3B:** DOJ pattern-or-practice policing record, federal prison/Guantánamo evidence, and protest treatment.
4. **4B:** Exact Fifth Circuit pages plus Supreme Court procedural history; press-access and executive-constraint evidence.
5. **5B:** BEA/BLS tables, CBO fiscal analysis, and independent IRA/CHIPS/IIJA implementation evaluations.
6. **6B:** CMS ACA enrollment, prescription-drug rollout, food insecurity, housing/homelessness, and education recovery.
7. **7B:** Exact Hur-report pages; congressional bank-record evidence and responses; ethics disclosures and appointment/conflict records.
8. **8B:** GAO implementation findings and milestone/outcome evidence across the frozen portfolio.

This research supports a usable but uneven eight-chapter dossier. Chapters 3B, 6B, and 8B have the strongest narrative yield; 1B, 2B, and especially 7B require targeted continuation before they are reasonably saturated.
