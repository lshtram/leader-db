Earlier reconnaissance:

**Overview**
Xi Jinping held power in China throughout 2023 as Communist Party general secretary, PRC president, and chairman of the Central Military Commission. Formally, the state presidency has limited constitutional powers; in practice, Xi’s authority was broader because the party controls the legislature, government appointments, courts, security organs, and the military. The main institutional limits were not independent checks in the liberal-democratic sense, but the need to operate through party-state structures, the State Council’s administrative machinery, and external constraints from the economy, foreign governments, and international markets.

- **Fact:** Xi Jinping was the top leader in 2023, combining party, state, and military leadership. He was re-elected president and CMC chairman in March 2023, while the legislature also endorsed the cabinet lineup and other top officials.
- **Why it matters:** This establishes who actually governed China during the period and shows that 2023 decisions were made from the apex of a highly centralized system.
