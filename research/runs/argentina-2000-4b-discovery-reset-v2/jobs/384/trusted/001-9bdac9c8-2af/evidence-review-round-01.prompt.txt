Highest-value unresolved themes:

1. **The 1999 transfer and inherited baseline**
   - Exact election-observer or contemporary report.
   - Whether the election and transfer were free, fair, accepted, and constitutionally routine.

2. **The 2000 Senate bribery controversy**
   - Contemporaneous congressional, prosecutorial, judicial, or reputable investigative sources.
   - Precise distinction among allegation, evidence, executive attribution, later acquittal or other disposition.
   - Its relevance to legislative independence and institutional manipulation.

3. **Protests and security-force conduct during 2000**
   - Exact event dates, deaths/injuries/arrests, responsible police level, executive authority, investigations, and remedies.
   - Federal versus provincial responsibility is essential.

4. **Media freedom**
   - Contemporary press-freedom report or human-rights report.
   - Government advertising, ownership pressure, defamation, threats, censorship, or access-to-information actions.

5. **Courts and appointments**
   - Judicial-reform policies, Supreme Court relations, appointment processes, compliance with decisions, and inherited Menem-era institutional conditions.

6. **Coalition and congressional relations**
   - Whether the Alianza government respected coalition commitments and legislative constraints.
   - Avoid converting ordinary coalition conflict into political-freedom evidence without a direct mechanism.

7. **Political equality**
   - Women, minorities, indigenous groups, excluded regions, and unpopular viewpoints.
   - Separate formal suffrage from practical equality.

8. **Surveillance and administrative harassment**
   - Contemporary rights reports or legal records.
   - Absence from current materials is a research gap, not an absence finding.

9. **Trajectory**
   - Comparable pre-2000 and end-of-2000 evidence.
   - Concrete changes enacted during the target year and their durability.

Preferred source families for continuation:

- the exact 2000 U.S. State Department report;
- Amnesty International annual reporting;
- Human Rights Watch annual reporting;
- Inter-American Commission on Human Rights material;
- Committee to Protect Journalists or equivalent contemporary press-freedom reporting;
- Argentine congressional and judicial records;
- reputable Argentine newspaper archives;
- academic histories focused on the Alianza government and institutional politics.
