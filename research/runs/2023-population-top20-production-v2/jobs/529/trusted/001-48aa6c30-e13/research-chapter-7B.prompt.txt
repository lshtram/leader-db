## Starting material

Earlier reconnaissance:

**Overview**

Ali Khamenei remained Iran’s supreme leader in 2023 and therefore the state’s top authority. Formally, the office controlled national policy, the armed forces, senior military appointments, the judiciary chief, state broadcasting, and key clerical/electoral bodies. In practice, the presidency and parliament operated under that framework, while the Guardian Council, judiciary, IRGC, and the Supreme Leader’s office constrained and filtered political competition. The year was marked by continuing fallout from the 2022 “Woman, Life, Freedom” protests, intensified repression and executions, a modest diplomatic thaw with Saudi Arabia, and an economy still shaped by sanctions, inflation, and structural fragility.

## Evidence Records

### 1. Supreme leadership was the real center of power, but exercised through institutions that also constrained politics
- **Underlying fact:** Khamenei’s office remained the decisive locus of authority in 2023, but it operated through a layered system rather than single-handed day-to-day rule.
- **Why it matters:** Any assessment of 2023 policy outcomes has to attribute final responsibility to the supreme leader while recognizing that implementation ran through the presidency, judiciary, IRGC, Guardian Council, and other institutions.
- **Strongest source:** [Council on Foreign Relations, “The Islamic Republic of Iran’s Power Centers”](https://www.cfr.org/articles/islamic-republics-power-centers), 2026-03 (background explainer; not period-specific but structurally relevant).
- **Supporting passage:** CFR states the supreme leader is “effectively Iran’s leader for life,” that Article 110 gives the office major powers, and that the leader commands the armed forces and appoints military chiefs and IRGC/police heads. It also notes the president serves under the leader and sets policy only within those parameters. [Source lines](#) from the opened page: [turn7view0](https://www.cfr.org/articles/islamic-republics-power-centers)
- **Period connection:** Inherited structure, active in 2023.
- **Connection strength to ruler/authority:** Direct and strong.
- **Complicating/contrary evidence:** The Assembly of Experts formally oversees the leader, but CFR notes this oversight is secretive and has not meaningfully checked Khamenei; that means formal checks existed but were weak in practice.
- **Cautions:** CFR is a secondary analytical source and should be read as a synthesis, not as the constitutional text itself.
- **Independent support:** [Iran Data Portal, Article 99 interpretation](https://irandataportal.syr.edu/interpretation-of-article-99); [Encyclopaedia Iranica, Guardian Council](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/guardian-council/).

## Workplan

1. **Orient.** Establish the ruler's formal and practical authority, the inherited situation, major external shocks or constraints, and the information environment.
2. **Plan coverage.** For every selected lens, identify the important favorable, adverse, disputed, and exculpatory possibilities that research should test.
3. **Find the main record.** Begin with strong overviews and syntheses. Identify the consequential decisions, laws, budgets, appointments, statements, implementation, corrections, and outcomes.
4. **Verify material claims.** Open the underlying sources. Use precise laws, official records, audits, judgments, datasets, scholarship, monitoring, and credible reporting where they establish or challenge an important claim. Assess each source’s authority, independence, method, proximity, and date.
5. **Test the account.** Look for contrary evidence, independent corroboration, relevant local-language material, source dependencies, and alternative explanations. Distinguish inherited conditions and country context from conduct attributable to the ruler.
6. **Deepen where useful.** Repeat steps 3–5 while new searches add a material fact, a stronger source, a missing perspective, or a necessary correction. Finish when further searching mainly repeats the established record or reaches a concrete access barrier.
7. **Prepare the handoff.** Organize the evidence by underlying fact and map each developed claim to every selected lens it supports.

- every selected question ID the claim supports.

Keep accepted evidence, corroboration, reused sources, uninspected leads, rejected sources, and blocked sources in clearly labelled groups. Explain the source landscape when a lens remains unanswered.
