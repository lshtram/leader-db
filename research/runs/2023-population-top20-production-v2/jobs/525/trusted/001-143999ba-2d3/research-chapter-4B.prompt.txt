## Starting material

Earlier reconnaissance:

**Overview**

Abdel Fattah el-Sisi was Egypt’s president in 2023 and the central executive authority in a system where the military, security services, and aligned state institutions dominated policymaking. Formally, he was head of state and supreme commander; in practice, parliament and most civilian checks had limited ability to constrain him. The period was defined by three interlocking features: a tightly controlled presidential election, severe repression of dissent, and worsening economic strain under inflation, power cuts, devaluation pressure, and IMF conditionality.

**Evidence Records**

1. **Formal office and real governing power**
- **Fact:** Sisi held the presidency, but actual power was concentrated in the presidency-security-military bloc rather than in parliament or ordinary civilian institutions.
- **Why it matters:** This is the baseline for attributing decisions and limits of authority.
- **Strongest source:** *Freedom House, Egypt: Freedom in the World 2023* (2023), https://freedomhouse.org/country/egypt/freedom-world/2023
- **Locator:** lines 73-87, 91-98, 156-157 in the opened page.
- **Support:** Freedom House states that since the 2013 coup the military and intelligence agencies dominate the political system; that Sisi and the security apparatus dominate policymaking; and that the 2019 amendments strengthened military influence and preserved the defense minister appointment mechanism. The official Egyptian presidency page also states the president is supreme commander of the armed forces and must obtain parliamentary approval for major war/treaty actions, which is a formal legal limit.
- **Period tag:** inherited condition plus 2023 application.
- **Connection strength to ruler:** very strong.
- **Complicating evidence:** the formal constitution still places some war/treaty/emergency steps under parliamentary or consultative approval.
- **Cautions:** Freedom House is an advocacy-based assessment, not a primary legal text; its wording is evaluative.
- **Independent support:** Reuters reporting on the 2023 election and the Egyptian state presidency/constitution text.

2. **2023 election as an uncontested ratification of rule**
- **Fact:** In December 2023 Sisi won a third term with 89.6% of the vote in an election in which he faced no serious challengers.
- **Why it matters:** It shows how power was maintained in 2023 and how constrained political competition had become.
- **Strongest source:** Reuters, *Egypt's Sisi sweeps to third term as president with 89.6% of vote* (18 Dec. 2023), https://www.investing.com/news/world-news/egypts-sisi-sweeps-to-third-term-as-president-with-896-of-vote-3258959
- **Locator:** lines 419-433 in the opened page.
- **Support:** Reuters reports the election was widely seen as foregone, that the state and tightly controlled media pushed turnout, and that security agencies prevented serious challengers from running. It also notes the Gaza war framed the campaign.
- **Period tag:** requested perio

## Workplan

1. **Orient.** Establish the ruler's formal and practical authority, the inherited situation, major external shocks or constraints, and the information environment.
2. **Plan coverage.** For every selected lens, identify the important favorable, adverse, disputed, and exculpatory possibilities that research should test.
3. **Find the main record.** Begin with strong overviews and syntheses. Identify the consequential decisions, laws, budgets, appointments, statements, implementation, corrections, and outcomes.
4. **Verify material claims.** Open the underlying sources. Use precise laws, official records, audits, judgments, datasets, scholarship, monitoring, and credible reporting where they establish or challenge an important claim. Assess each source’s authority, independence, method, proximity, and date.
5. **Test the account.** Look for contrary evidence, independent corroboration, relevant local-language material, source dependencies, and alternative explanations. Distinguish inherited conditions and country context from conduct attributable to the ruler.
6. **Deepen where useful.** Repeat steps 3–5 while new searches add a material fact, a stronger source, a missing perspective, or a necessary correction. Finish when further searching mainly repeats the established record or reaches a concrete access barrier.
7. **Prepare the handoff.** Organize the evidence by underlying fact and map each developed claim to every selected lens it supports.

## Evidence records

Create one record for one source supporting one material claim. If one source supports several materially different claims used downstream, create a separate record and locator for each claim. Give records about the same underlying fact or event the same `underlying_fact_key`, and identify reports that depend on the same investigation, dataset, wire story, or official claim.

Each developed record includes:

- every selected question ID the claim supports.

## Discovery/extraction boundary

A separate discovery stage has already built the candidate source catalogue shown in Resources already identified. This turn is evidence extraction and gap-directed inspection, not the primary broad-discovery pass. Evidence-unit targets are depth diagnostics and must not be used as proof that the source universe is saturated.
