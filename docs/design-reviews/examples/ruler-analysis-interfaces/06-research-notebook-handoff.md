# Research notebook handoff — representative shape

This is a compact illustration of the permissive notebook passed from the persistent
researcher to the evidence reviewer. It is not a scored result and not a strict dossier.

## Case

- Run: `2000-scale-pilot-5-v1`
- Country: Argentina (`ARG`)
- Ruler: Fernando de la Rúa (`ruler_year_id=2414`)
- Period: 2000
- Chapters: 1B through 8B

## Local-prior audit

- `LF001`: retained as country-year conflict context for 2B; not direct ruler attribution.
- Nuclear prior rows: no evidence found; absence is not responsible conduct.
- Governance and welfare facts: retained as inherited baselines only.

## Global evidence ledger

### E001 — target-period policy or event

- Claim: A precisely bounded claim attributable to the ruler or clearly labeled as context.
- Source: underlying source title and URL.
- Locator: page, section, paragraph, table, or local-prior locator.
- Period fit: direct 2000 evidence.
- Attribution: explains ruler authority, choice, knowledge, tolerance, or limitation.
- Contrary material: records material evidence against the provisional interpretation.
- Candidate mappings: `2B.1`, `2B.3`, `8B.10`.

## Chapter pass

### 1B

- Candidate sources inspected: recorded even when rejected.
- Accepted evidence: none or explicit ledger IDs.
- Remaining gaps: target-year safeguards, doctrine, safety, command, or posture decisions.

### 2B

- Reused ledger IDs: `E001`, …
- New targeted discovery: event, diplomacy, legal role, contrary accounts.
- Remaining attribution gaps: explicit and recoverable where possible.

_The live notebook continues through all eight chapters and checkpoints queries, sources,
accepted/rejected candidates, mappings, gaps, and execution profile._
