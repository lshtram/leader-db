# AMLO 2022 Preflight Score and Attribution Audit

## Decision

The AMLO case is cleared as a pipeline preflight for the controlled full evaluation.
The original experimental 2B judgment is rejected. Its corrected replacement is 6.0
with 78 confidence and a 5.5–6.5 range. The four other manual-review flags are resolved
as ordinary uncertainty for evaluation purposes; their scores remain provisional and
will be recalibrated in the full 20-ruler common-meter batches.

This audit does not promote any AMLO score into a production release.

## 2B — correction required and completed

The original 5.5 judgment gave adverse directional weight to National Guard
militarization and domestic public-security accountability (`E030`, `E039`–`E043`).
The active 2B guide explicitly says ordinary domestic policing and repression belong
in 3B and requires documented target-period international conduct.

The corrected singleton judge gave those records zero 2B scoring weight. It used AMLO's five-year
truce and international peace-committee proposal (`E036`), Mexico's mediation and
peaceful-resolution advocacy (`E037`), and Mexico's condemnation of Russia's invasion,
UN vote, refusal of weapons, and non-sanctions neutrality (`E045`). It did not reward
the absence-of-aggression gap (`E038`). A 6.0 is defensible: there was real,
ruler-attributed diplomatic restraint, but limited leverage, credibility, and no
demonstrated peace outcome. Its empty `calibrated_against` list means that 6.0 is a
defensible singleton preflight result, not a completed common-meter cohort judgment.
No preflight blocker remains; the full chapter batch must recalibrate it.

## 4B — material attribution flag resolved for evaluation

The 5.0 judgment rests on ruler-attributed pressure on electoral institutions and
critics (`E056`–`E061`) balanced against functioning electoral, judicial, and
legislative constraints (`E023`, `E027`, `E029`, `E064`–`E066`). It explicitly does
not attribute unresolved Pegasus authorization or every security abuse to AMLO
(`E062`, `E063`). Those gaps affect confidence and the 4.5–6.0 range, which still
permits material movement. The record is nevertheless judgeable, so the flag is
cleared only as a preflight execution blocker. The 5.0 remains provisional and the
full common-meter batch must resolve its ordering and attribution.

## 5B — material attribution flag resolved for evaluation

The 5.5 judgment uses attributable fiscal and regulatory choices plus independently
reported household, investment, inflation, and labor outcomes (`E006`, `E068`–`E075`).
It separates external inflation shocks and central-bank autonomy from AMLO attribution.
Uncertainty about appointment independence, procurement, and policy correction is
already reflected in 68 confidence and the 5.0–6.0 range. It does not presently support
a one-point change, so the publication-style flag is not an evaluation blocker.

## 7B — personal-nexus boundary passes

The 4.5 judgment relies on direct personal-nexus evidence: sampled false or misleading
presidential claims (`E086`, `E087`) and AMLO's personal response to a critical
journalist (`E091`). It does not convert national corruption or unproven family
conflicts into personal misconduct; the unresolved and contrary records are preserved
(`E088`, `E089`, `E098`, `E099`). The score is therefore judgeable under the strict
7B boundary. Remaining uncertainty about intent, correction, and family nexus supports
the 3.5–5.5 range, not a release-blocking flag at the evaluation stage.

## 8B — program and attribution chain passes

The 6.0 judgment identifies an explicit governing program and ruler-owned reforms,
then links them to implementation and independent evaluation (`E096`, `E100`, `E101`).
It weighs execution weaknesses and mixed security outcomes (`E002`, `E004`, `E103`,
`E105`) and separates moral approval from competence. Personnel quality, adaptation,
and causal attribution remain incomplete, but the record contains enough program,
implementation, and outcome evidence to support the score and range. The flag is
ordinary uncertainty for evaluation, not a blocker.
